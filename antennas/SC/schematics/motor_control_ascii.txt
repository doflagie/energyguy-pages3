MOTOR OVERVIEW:
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The 28BYJ-48 is a 5V geared unipolar stepper motor with integral 64:1 gearbox.
Specifications:
  Step angle (full step):  5.625° / 64 gear ratio = 0.0879° per output step
  Steps per output rev:    64 × (360/5.625) = 4096 full steps / 2048 full revs
  Half-stepping:           4096 × 2 = 8192 half-steps per output revolution
  Rated voltage:           5V
  Phase resistance:        ~50Ω per phase
  No-load current:         ~100 mA per phase
  Holding torque:          300–400 gf·cm
  Connector:               5-pin JST PH2.0 (WHT=5V, BLK=IN4, GRN=IN3, YEL=IN2, ORG=IN1)

NOTE: The 28BYJ-48 has 4 coil phases driven by the ULN2003. The pink/white wire
(pin 5) is the 5V center tap — connect to +5V, NOT to ESP32 GPIO.

DRIVE MECHANICS:
  Motor output shaft → 5mm jaw coupler → 6.35mm jaw coupler → 1/4-20 threaded rod
  1/4-20 rod: 20 TPI = 1.27 mm linear travel per rod revolution
  Steps per winding turn (half-step): (2.3 mm / 1.27 mm/rev) × 8192 = 14,840 half-steps
  NOTE: firmware uses STEPS_PER_COIL_TURN = 7420 (half-steps as defined in code)
        VERIFY empirically: wind wiper from known position to measured position

================================================================================
  SCHEMATIC — LIMIT SWITCHES
================================================================================

  HOME SWITCH (bottom of wiper travel — minimum inductance position):

  +3.3V ──[10kΩ]──┬──── GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6, input only)
                   │
                  [SPDT microswitch]  ← wiper bracket actuates plunger
                   │  (Normally Open contact)
                  GND

  When wiper reaches bottom: switch closes → GPIO 34 reads LOW → home confirmed.
  Firmware: homeMotor() moves DOWN until PIN_HOME_SW == LOW; sets g_motor_pos = 0.

  TOP SWITCH (optional over-travel protection):

  +3.3V ──[10kΩ]──┬──── GPIO 36 (input only)
                   │
                  [SPDT microswitch]  ← actuated at maximum coil extension
                   │
                  GND

  Firmware checks GPIO 36 during upward movement; halts if triggered.

================================================================================
  HALF-STEP SEQUENCE TABLE — 28BYJ-48
================================================================================

  Half-stepping provides 2× resolution and smoother motion vs. full step.
  Sequence repeats every 8 steps. g_step_phase cycles 0→7→0.

  Phase  | IN1(GPIO25) | IN2(GPIO26) | IN3(GPIO27) | IN4(GPIO14) | Direction
  ───────┼─────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┼──────────
    0    |      1      |      0      |      0      |      0      | ↑ UP
    1    |      1      |      1      |      0      |      0      |
    2    |      0      |      1      |      0      |      0      |
    3    |      0      |      1      |      1      |      0      |
    4    |      0      |      0      |      1      |      0      |
    5    |      0      |      0      |      1      |      1      |
    6    |      0      |      0      |      0      |      1      |
    7    |      1      |      0      |      0      |      1      | ↓ DOWN

  For UP direction: sequence steps 0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,0,1,...
  For DOWN direction: sequence steps 7,6,5,4,3,2,1,0,7,6,...

  Step timing:
    Homing speed:    HOME_SPEED_DELAY = 3 ms/step  (slow, reliable)
    Band change:     FAST_SPEED_DELAY = 2 ms/step  (fastest reliable)
    Auto-tune:       TUNE_SPEED_DELAY = 5 ms/step  (slow for SWR stability)
    Min reliable:    2 ms/step (below this = missed steps likely)
    Max time (full sweep 40m→80m): ~57,000 steps × 5ms = 285 seconds if fine-tuning

================================================================================
  LED STATUS CODES
================================================================================

  LED Pattern                        | Meaning
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Status LED: fast blink (100ms)     | Homing in progress
  Status LED: double blink (500ms)   | Auto-tuning in progress
  Status LED: solid ON               | Idle; system ready
  Status LED: solid OFF              | No RF or firmware crash

  SWR LEDs (exclusive):
  Green solid                        | SWR ≤ 1.5:1 (GOOD)
  Yellow solid                       | SWR 1.5–2.5:1 (ACCEPTABLE)
  Red solid                          | SWR > 2.5:1 (RETUNE NEEDED)
  All LEDs off                       | No RF detected; awaiting signal

================================================================================
  SERIAL DIAGNOSTIC OUTPUT FORMAT
================================================================================

  ESP32 Serial monitor (115200 baud) outputs one line per measurement cycle:
  Format:
  SC|BAND:40m|POS:115306|TURNS:15.56|L:19.27uH|EFF:9.5%|SWR:1.42|FWD:5.00W|STATE:LOCKED

  Fields:
    BAND    : current band name
    POS     : motor step count from home
    TURNS   : calculated wiper position in coil turns
    L       : estimated inductance from Wheeler formula
    EFF     : antenna efficiency percent for this band
    SWR     : current measured SWR (or "---" if no RF)
    FWD     : forward power in watts
    STATE   : IDLE / HOMING / MOVING / SWEEPING / LOCKED
