1.0  WHIP PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

  Total extended length:    5.60 m  (7 × ~0.80 m nominal sections)
  Collapsed length:        ~0.36 m  (fits in field kit)
  Number of sections:       7
  Section material:         Stainless steel or aluminum alloy tubing
  Nominal section length:   0.800 m each (see exact lengths below)
  Mount:                    3/8-24 threaded stud (standard CB/ham mobile)

  SECTION DESIGNATIONS (from base toward tip):

    Sec 1: Base section (largest diameter, 3/8-24 threaded base)
    Sec 2: Second section
    Sec 3: Third section
    Sec 4: Fourth section
    Sec 5: Fifth section
    Sec 6: Sixth section
    Sec 7: Tip section (smallest diameter)

  All sections retract into Sec 1 when collapsed.
  Measurement references are from the BASE of Sec 1 (mounting point).

  NOTES ON 20m OPERATION:
    The 5.60 m whip is approximately 10% too long for 20m resonance.
    Two methods:
      a) Retract Sec7 (tip) until total length = ~5.05 m.
         Sec7 has a mark at 5.05 m from base (55 mm extended from Sec6).
      b) Leave whip at full 5.60 m and add a 300 pF series capacitor at the
         feed point to introduce a small capacitive detuning — simpler in field.

  NOTES ON 17m OPERATION:
    Extending exactly 5 sections (Sec1–Sec5) gives 4.00 m.
    This is very close to λ/4 at 17m (3.99 m).  No further adjustment needed.
    Fine-tune ±20 mm on tip of Sec5.

  EXTENSION PROCEDURE BY BAND:

    160m / 80m / 60m / 40m / 30m:
      ► Extend all 7 sections to full length (5.60 m).
      ► Install appropriate loading coil module at base.
      ► Adjust shunt capacitor for minimum SWR on target frequency.

    20m:
      ► Extend all 7 sections.
      ► Retract Sec 7 until tip is at the 20m mark (5.05 m from base).
        — OR — extend fully and connect 300 pF series capacitor at base.
      ► No loading coil required.

    17m:
      ► Extend Sec 1 through Sec 5 fully.
      ► Leave Sec 6 and Sec 7 retracted inside Sec 5.
      ► Total height = 4.00 m from base.
      ► No loading coil.

    15m:
      ► Extend Sec 1 through Sec 5.
      ► Retract Sec 5 tip to 15m mark (3.43 m total from base).
      ► Or: extend Sec 1–Sec 4 fully (3.20 m), then extend Sec 5 by 0.23 m.

    12m:
      ► Extend Sec 1 through Sec 4.
      ► Retract Sec 4 tip to 12m mark (2.92 m total from base).

    10m:
      ► Extend Sec 1 through Sec 4.
      ► Retract Sec 4 to 10m mark (2.55 m total from base).

    6m:
      ► Extend Sec 1 through Sec 2 fully.
      ► Tip of Sec 2 sits at 1.60 m — retract slightly to 1.40 m mark.
      ► Leave Sec 3 through Sec 7 retracted.

    2m:
      ► Extend Sec 1 only to 2m mark (0.488 m from base).
      ► All other sections retracted.
      ► Mount must clear the base loading unit hardware by ~50 mm.

    70cm:
      ► Extend Sec 1 to 70cm mark (0.160 m from base).
      ► This is a stub operation; for best results use a dedicated
        VHF/UHF collinear — the whip is marginal at 432 MHz.

5.0  MARK APPLICATION METHOD
================================================================================

  OPTION A — PAINT RINGS (preferred for portable field use):

    Material:   Nail varnish or enamel model paint, 2mm wide ring
    Apply:      Clean section with isopropyl alcohol first.
                Apply ring at measured position using a piece of tape
                as a guide.  Two coats for durability.
    Protect:    Seal completed marks with clear nail varnish top coat.

    COLOR CODE:
      70cm:   White ring + black "70" lettering
      1.25m:  Yellow ring + black "125"
      2m:     Green ring + black "2M"
      6m:     Orange ring + black "6M"
      10m:    Red ring + black "10"
      12m:    Red-orange ring + black "12"
      15m:    Blue ring + white "15"
      17m:    Blue ring + white "17" (at tip of Sec 5 = full extension)
      20m:    Dashed white ring + black "20" (Sec 7 retraction point)

  OPTION B — ENGRAVED MARKS:

    Tool:     Dremel with fine engraving bit, or scriber
    Depth:    0.3 mm max to avoid weakening wall
    After:    Fill engraved groove with contrasting paint, wipe flush
    Note:     Not suitable for thin-wall sections (Sec 5–7)

  OPTION C — TAPE FLAGS (temporary field expedient):

    Material:   Colored electrical tape, 10 mm strip wrapped around
    Note:       Will slip over time; verify position periodically

  TOLERANCE:   Mark positions should be accurate to ±5 mm.
               This corresponds to less than ±0.5% frequency error
               on most bands, well within tuning range.

================================================================================
6.0  OPERATING NOTES
================================================================================

  LOCKING SECTIONS:
    ► Each section has a friction lock (knurled collar or spring clip).
    ► Extend firmly to fully seated position; twist to lock if collar-type.
    ► Verify all extended sections are locked before transmitting.
    ► Partial section extension (to a mark) must be locked at that position.

  WIND LOADING:
    ► 5.60 m fully extended is structurally marginal in wind > 30 km/h.
    ► Reduce to 5 sections (4.00 m) in moderate wind; use loading coil.
    ► Always guy the base or hold-down when operating portable.

  COUNTERPOISE REMINDER:
    ► On HF bands with loading coil: deploy all 4 counterpoise wires.
    ► On VHF/UHF: no counterpoise needed for ground-plane mount;
      3 × λ/4 radials on the base stud improve pattern on 6m and 2m.

  BIRD STRIKES AND SHARP TIP:
    ► The tip section (Sec 7) can be extremely sharp.
    ► Fit a rubber tip cap when transporting or during non-use.
    ► Remove tip cap before transmitting.

  STORAGE:
    ► Collapse fully; wipe with light oil (CRC 5-56 or equivalent).
    ► Store in included nylon sleeve to protect finish.
    ► Do not overtighten collapsed sections — leave slight slack.

  FIELD QUICK-REFERENCE CARD:
    ► A laminated wallet card with the band/extension table from Sec 2.0
      should be affixed inside the lid of the base-loading-coil box.
