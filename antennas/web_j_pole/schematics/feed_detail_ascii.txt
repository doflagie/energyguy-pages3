1. PORTABLE HF CLIP CONNECTOR (160m – 10m)
=============================================

   MATERIALS:
   - 2× insulated alligator clips (banana-plug type, fully insulated jaw)
   - RG-316 or RG-174 miniature coax (flexible)
   - PL-259 or BNC connector at radio end
   - 3× FT-43 ferrite beads threaded on coax at feed point

   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  STRIP A (FRONT FACE):                                        │
   │  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐   │
   │  │ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓ FRONT TAPE ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓ │   │
   │  │         ┌───────┐                                    │   │
   │  │         │CLIP A │──────────────────── coax CENTER   │   │
   │  │         └───────┘                                    │   │
   │  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘   │
   │                      ↑ d_feed from bottom                     │
   │  STRIP B (BACK FACE):                                         │
   │  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐   │
   │  │         ┌───────┐                                    │   │
   │  │         │CLIP B │──────────────────── coax SHIELD   │   │
   │  │         └───────┘                                    │   │
   │  │ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓ BACK TAPE ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  │   │
   │  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘   │
   │                                                               │
   │  FERRITE CHOKE: 5 turns of coax through FT-43 core,          │
   │  immediately below the clip connection (within 50mm).         │
   └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   ADJUSTMENT PROCEDURE:
   1. Attach both clips at calculated d_feed (NEC starting point from table).
   2. Sweep antenna with NanoVNA (0.5× to 1.5× target frequency).
   3. Move both clips TOGETHER (maintain same height) up or down.
   4. Minimum S11 reflection = optimal feed point.
   5. Mark height with Sharpie; slide clips to this mark for each use.


2. SEMI-PERMANENT VHF TAB CONNECTOR (6m – 2m)
================================================

   MATERIALS:
   - 50mm × 15mm copper foil tape, 2 pieces
   - RG-58 or RG-174 coax
   - BNC female chassis connector
   - Isopropyl alcohol for cleaning
   - Rosin-core solder (low-temp, 60/40 or 63/37)

   CONSTRUCTION:

   Step 1 — Prepare anchor tabs:
   Cut two 50mm × 15mm copper foil tabs.

   Step 2 — Pre-tin the coax end:
   Strip coax: 20mm outer jacket, 15mm braid, 10mm inner dielectric.
   Pre-tin center conductor and braid shield separately.

   Step 3 — Clean tape surface at feed point:
   Wipe with IPA. Do not touch afterward.

   Step 4 — Solder center conductor tab:
   Lay the copper tab across Strip A (front face) at d_feed.
   Solder center conductor to the tab.
   The tab's adhesive bonds it to the tape surface; solder provides electrical connection.

   Step 5 — Solder shield tab:
   Apply second tab to Strip B (back face) at same height.
   Solder braid shield to the tab.

    │  STRIP A           │        │  STRIP B           │
    │  ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓ │        │  ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓ │
    │  ──────────────── │        │  ──────────────── │

3. PCB-MOUNT CONNECTOR (70cm – 23cm)
=======================================

   At 70cm and above, the coax lead from the feed point to the connector
   must be ZERO LENGTH or as short as possible. Any coax stub becomes part
   of the antenna. Use a chassis-mount connector soldered DIRECTLY to the tape.

   MATERIALS:
   - SMA female right-angle PCB connector
   - 0.025-inch hobby brass sheet (backing), 25mm × 25mm
   - Fine-gauge solder (0.5mm or smaller)

   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │         CROSS-SECTION AT FEED POINT (UHF):              │
   │                                                         │
   │    ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓ STRIP A ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓          │
   │    ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░ WEBBING ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░          │
   │    ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓ STRIP B ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓          │
   │            │                   │                         │
   │         CENTER               SHIELD                      │
   │          TAB                   TAB                       │
   │         (10mm)               (10mm)                      │
   │            │                   │                         │
   │         SMA pin             SMA body                     │
   │            └─────── SMA ────────┘                        │
   │                   (right angle)                           │
   │                   → coax to radio                        │
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   INSTALLATION:
   1. Cut 10mm notch through webbing at d_feed to expose tape on both faces.
   2. Extend a 10mm tab of strip A copper through the notch.
   3. Extend a 10mm tab of strip B copper through the notch.
   4. Solder SMA center pin to Strip A tab, SMA body/ground to Strip B tab.
   5. Seal with RTV on back side.
   6. At 70cm: acceptable coax lead < 15mm.
   7. At 23cm: solder directly, lead < 8mm, no flexible coax if possible.


4. COMMON-MODE CHOKE (ALL BANDS)
==================================

   The J-pole feed point is at the current minimum of the radiating element,
   but there is still coax current possible on the outer shield if no choke
   is used. This causes pattern distortion and SWR variation when the hand
   or body touches the feedline.

   REQUIRED: ferrite common-mode choke at the feed point.

   HF CHOKE (1.8 – 30 MHz):
   - 10 turns of RG-58 on FT-240-43 toroid (2.4-inch OD, gray)
   - Place within 50mm of the feed point clips
   - Loop all coax around the same side of the toroid
   - Do NOT overlap turns; single layer

   VHF CHOKE (50 – 148 MHz):
   - 5 turns of RG-58 on FT-240-43 toroid
   - Or: 6 ferrite beads (73 material) threaded on coax

   UHF CHOKE (146 – 450 MHz):
   - 5 ferrite beads (43 material) threaded on coax
   - Or: λ/4 coax sleeve (choke sleeve):
     Cut a 50mm piece of copper tape; fold lengthwise into a tube around coax;
     one end at feed point (connected to shield), other end open (sleeve length = λ/4)

   SLEEVE CHOKE DIMENSIONS:
   Band  │  λ/4 at freq (mm)  │  VF_sleeve × λ/4 (mm)
   ──────┼─────────────────────┼───────────────────────
   2m    │  146 MHz → 513mm   │  VF=0.66 → 339mm (coax wrapped in Al foil)
   70cm  │  446 MHz → 168mm   │  VF=0.66 → 111mm
   33cm  │  906 MHz → 83mm    │  VF=0.66 → 55mm

   For sleeve choke: leave open end bare; connect closed end to coax shield.
   The sleeve resonates at the design frequency and presents high impedance to
   common-mode current at that point.


5. FEED POINT ADJUSTMENT TOOL
================================

   Build a simple adjustment aid from a bent paper clip or bobby pin:
   - Two metal fingers bent to span both faces of the webbing
   - Length = d_feed estimated range (30–50mm for HF)
   - Mark positions at 5mm intervals on the tool with paint pen

   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │         FEED-POINT SLIDER TOOL:                  │
   │                                                  │
   │  FRONT                  BACK                     │
   │  FACE                   FACE                     │
   │   │                     │                        │
   │   ├──────────────────────┤  ← METAL BRIDGE       │
   │   │ •  •  •  •  •  •  • │  ← 5mm marks          │
   │   │ 5  10 15 20 25 30 35 │    (position in mm    │
   │   │                      │     from bottom)      │
   │   coax center         coax shield                 │
   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   Clip to the slider, slide along the webbing surface, read position from marks.
   When SWR minimum found: transfer mark to webbing permanently.
