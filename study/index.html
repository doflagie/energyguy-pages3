About the Question Pool

This quiz uses the practice questions from the Merv's Dump Ham Radio Study Guide. The questions cover all three sections of the Technician exam:

  • Frequency Privileges - Band limits, modes, power
  • Operating Practices - Procedures, Q-codes, repeaters
  • FCC Rules - Regulations, identification, prohibited activities
