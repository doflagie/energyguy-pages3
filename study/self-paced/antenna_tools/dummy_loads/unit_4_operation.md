## Chapter 5 Content

1. Always confirm dummy load is connected before transmitting. A
 momentary key-down into open coax can damage the final transistor.
2. For CW/SSB testing: key down for ≤10 seconds, off for ≥30 seconds
 until thermal equilibrium is reached (approximately 15 minutes at full power).
3. For the oil loads: after extended use, check for oil seepage at the
 lid seal. If oil temperature exceeds 70°C (hot to the touch), reduce
 duty cycle or allow cooling.
4. Never use the 100W dry load above 100W continuous; use the oil loads
 for higher power or high duty cycle modes (FT8, WSPR, digital).

## Operating Notes

Chapter 5 specifies 4 operating steps.

Measurement discipline:
- Always warm up the tool and reference sources before taking readings
- Multiple readings improve accuracy — use 3–5 readings and average them
- Record every reading with frequency, power level, and settings
- A result that seems wrong almost always means a setup error, not a failed tool
