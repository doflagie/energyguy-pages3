Self-Paced Course — DISCONE CALIBRATION (HOMEBREW)

TM-ANT-002 — Open Handout TM-ANT-002 Estimated time: 2–3 hours (reading + practice + field lab) Handout: TM-ANT-002 (discone/tm.html) — keep it open throughout

What You Will Be Able to Do

TLO-1: Given the required materials and tools — construct the DISCONE CALIBRATION (HOMEBREW) per TM-ANT-002, tune it to resonance, and verify performance meets the SWR and resonance criteria stated in Chapter 7.

TLO-2: Given NanoVNA or SWR bridge measurements — interpret resonance and SWR data, apply the required calculations from Appendix A, and determine pass/fail against the stated acceptance criterion.

Scope

the DISCONE CALIBRATION (HOMEBREW).

How to Use This Course

  1. Work units in order.
  2. For each unit: read the assigned TM chapter first, then read the unit, then answer the self-check questions.
  3. Self-check answers are at the bottom of each unit — try before looking.
  4. The checkpoint at the end of each unit is a go/no-go.
  5. Complete the Unit 4 and Unit 5 labs with a real antenna before taking the final assessment.
  6. Passing score: 13/15 (87%). All calculation questions must be correct regardless of total score.
