CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers construction of a precision decade resistance box and fixed resistor standards for calibrating ohmmeters, resistance bridges, and ADC reference circuits. Resistors are available with ±0.01% tolerance and 2 ppm/°C TC from Vishay and similar vendors.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. RESISTOR TYPES

For precision standards, use bulk metal foil (BMF) or metal film resistors. Wire-wound resistors are excellent for DC but introduce inductance at AC. Carbon film and carbon composition types are inadequate for calibration use due to high TC and poor long-term stability.

2-2. 4-WIRE KELVIN CONNECTIONS

For resistances below 100 Ω, contact resistance in connectors and switch contacts introduces significant error. Use 4-wire (Kelvin) connections: separate current-force and voltage-sense terminals for each resistor position. See TM-CAL-010 for full Kelvin procedure.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Mount resistors in each decade: 1Ω–9Ω (nine 1Ω), 10Ω–90Ω (nine 10Ω), etc.
  2. Use low-thermal-EMF solder (SAC305 or rosin-flux silver solder). Avoid no-clean flux which may cause thermal EMF at solder joints.
  3. Keep lead lengths short and symmetrical within each decade.
  4. Wire switch wipers in series: decade 4 wiper → decade 3 input → decade 2 input → decade 1 input → output terminal.
  5. Verify each decade with ohmmeter before final assembly.
  6. Mount in enclosure with clear dial markings.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Measure each individual resistor value before assembly. Record actual values.
  2. After assembly, measure at each switch position using Fluke 15B+ or better DMM.
  3. For positions <10 Ω, use 4-wire Kelvin measurement (TM-CAL-010).
  4. Record actual vs. nominal value at each position in calibration table.
  5. Apply correction factors in use: Vactual = switch setting × (Rmeas / Rnominal).

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

NOTEDecade boxes are not adjustable after construction. Select resistors with measured values closest to nominal at time of assembly. Keep spares for replacement if any resistor drifts.

CHAPTER 7 — VERIFICATION

  1. Verify 1 Ω, 10 Ω, 100 Ω, 1k Ω, 10k Ω positions against NIST-certified reference resistors if available.
  2. For everyday use: compare to Wheatstone bridge null measurement (TM-CAL-009).
  3. Log calibration values; re-check annually.
