SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

FREQUENCY COVERAGE:
- HF Section: 1.8 - 30 MHz (160m through 10m bands)
- VHF/UHF Section: 50 - 1300 MHz (6m through 20cm bands)

POWER RANGE:
- Minimum: 0.1 W (QRP)
- Maximum: 1500 W (HF), 500 W (VHF/UHF)
- Ranges: 1W / 10W / 100W / 1000W (switchable)

ACCURACY:
- After calibration: ±5% typical, ±10% worst case
- Temperature stability: ±0.5 dB over 0-50°C
- Frequency flatness: ±1 dB (HF), ±2 dB (VHF/UHF)

PHYSICAL:
- Size: 160mm × 100mm × 50mm (compact, portable)
- Weight: ~300g (analog), ~350g (digital)
- Power: 9V battery (20-40 hours digital, infinite analog)
- Connectors: SO-239 (UHF) or N-type optional

INSERTION LOSS:
- < 0.1 dB (negligible impact on transmitter or antenna)
