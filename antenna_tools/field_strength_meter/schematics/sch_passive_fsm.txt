================================================================================
SCHEMATIC: PASSIVE FIELD STRENGTH METER
TM-FSM-PASSIVE  Rev A
Germanium Diode Detector, Analog Meter Output
Frequency Range: 100 kHz – 500 MHz (broadband, untuned)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Passive (battery-free) field strength meter using a germanium point-contact
diode to envelope-detect received RF. Output drives a 100 μA FSD moving-coil
panel meter. Designed for relative field strength comparison at HF/VHF/UHF.
No amplification; relies on probe antenna to develop sufficient voltage.
Minimum detectable field (50 cm whip): approximately 0.5 mV/m at 7 MHz.

METER SHUNT (if M1 is 50 μA FSD):
    Add R_shunt across M1 to scale:
    R_shunt = (V_det_max - V_meter_FSD) / I_meter_FSD
    For Vdet_max ≈ 1V, I=50μA: R_shunt = 1V / 50μA = 20 kΩ
    (Adjust R_shunt empirically for desired FSD level)


OPTIONAL BAND-SWITCHED TUNED CIRCUIT
--------------------------------------

    Band switch S1 (1-pole 10-position rotary):

        Position 1: 160M  L=10μH, C=560pF  (parallel LC trap)
        Position 2: 80M   L=4.7μH, C=270pF
        Position 3: 40M   L=2.2μH, C=150pF
        Position 4: 30M   L=1μH, C=75pF
        Position 5: 20M   L=560nH, C=47pF
        Position 6: 15M   L=330nH, C=33pF
        Position 7: 10M   L=220nH, C=20pF
        Position 8: 6M    L=100nH, C=10pF
        Position 9: 2M    L=33nH, C=5pF
        Position 10: WIDE (bypass, all bands via broadband path)

    Each position connects the probe to an LC parallel resonant circuit
    tuned to band center. This provides bandpass selectivity (~6 dB/octave
    rolloff), improving sensitivity to the desired band and rejecting
    strong out-of-band signals that could saturate the detector.

    LC values provide resonance: f = 1/(2π√LC)

    Inductor construction (toroid for high Q):
      160M: T-50-6, 31 turns #26 AWG ≈ 10 μH
      80M:  T-37-6, 21 turns #26 AWG ≈ 4.7 μH
      40M:  T-37-6, 15 turns #26 AWG ≈ 2.2 μH
      20M:  T-37-6,  9 turns #26 AWG ≈ 560 nH
      10M:  T-37-6,  6 turns #26 AWG ≈ 220 nH
      2M:   T-25-6,  4 turns #28 AWG ≈ 33 nH (air coil acceptable)


GERMANIUM DIODE DETECTOR NOTES
---------------------------------

    D1 = 1N34A (preferred) or 1N60, OA47, OA90, AAZ15
         Germanium point-contact or Schottky (not silicon; Vf too high)
         Forward voltage: ~0.2–0.3V at low current
         Works at RF levels below 100 mVpp (silicon needs ~300 mVpp)

    Detector output:
      Vdet ≈ Vrf_peak - Vf_diode
      For Vrf = 50 mV peak: Vdet ≈ 50 mV - 30 mV = 20 mV (weak signal)
      For Vrf = 500 mV peak: Vdet ≈ 500 mV - 30 mV = 470 mV (strong)

    NOTE: The detector is square-law (Vout ∝ Power) for very small
    signals (Vrf < ~50 mV) and linear (Vout ∝ Vrf) for larger signals.
    The meter scale is therefore nonlinear; calibration points are needed.


SENSITIVITY / PROBE SELECTION
--------------------------------

    Probe type       Minimum detectable   Useful range
    ----------       ------------------   ------------
    50 cm whip       ~1 mV/m at 7 MHz     S5 signals
    Dipole (λ/2)     ~0.1 mV/m at 7 MHz  S3 signals (connected)
    Loop 10 cm       ~5 mV/m at 7 MHz     Near-field only
    Small loop 2M+   ~0.1 mV/m at 145MHz  VHF near-field

    To detect typical 100W station at 1 km distance on 40M:
    Free-space E ≈ 5.5 V/m at 1 km from 100W dipole
    Actual: much less (terrain, buildings); expect 0.1–1 V/m nearby
