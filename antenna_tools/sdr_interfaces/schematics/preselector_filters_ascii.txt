OVERVIEW
--------
  16 switchable bandpass filters (one per ham band) providing:
  - Rejection of out-of-band signals and intermodulation sources
  - Improved dynamic range for SDR front-end
  - FM broadcast (88–108 MHz) rejection when tuned to HF
  - Harmonic suppression from upconverter

  Architecture: SP4T × 4 cascaded relay tree = 16-position switch
  Control: 4-to-16 decoder (74HC154) driven by ESP32 GPIO[3:0]

FILTER TOPOLOGY — 3-POLE CHEBYSHEV BPF
-----------------------------------------
  Standard form (series resonator — shunt resonator — series resonator):

       Cs1      Ls1      Cs2      Ls2      Cs3      Ls3
  IN ──┤├──┬────UUU────┬──┤├──┬────UUU────┬──┤├──┬────UUU────┬── OUT
          │            │       │            │       │            │
         Lp1          Cp1     Lp2          Cp2     Lp3          Cp3
          │            │       │            │       │            │
         GND          GND     GND          GND     GND          GND

  Each section: series LC (Cs, Ls) and shunt LC (Lp, Cp) resonating at f0.
  Coupling between sections by series capacitors Cs1, Cs2, Cs3.

  Simplified 3-pole shorthand:
    - Series coupling caps: Cs (controls bandwidth coupling factor k)
    - Shunt resonators: Lp||Cp resonating at f0
    The three shunt resonators tune to f0; coupling caps set bandwidth.

COMPONENT VALUES — HF BANDPASS FILTERS
-----------------------------------------
  All values ±5%. Use NP0/C0G capacitors. Wind inductors on T50-2 or T50-6 toroids.
  After assembly, align with NanoVNA for S21 minimum passband loss.

  F01 — 160M (1.9 MHz, BW 200 kHz, Q=9.5):
    Shunt resonators:
      Lp = 22 µH (T50-2, ~75 turns 24 AWG)
      Cp = 320 pF (220 + 100 pF NP0 in parallel)
    Series coupling capacitors:
      Cs = 39 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~1 dB
    Note: winding 75 turns is tedious; use T80-2 or T94-2 for easier winding.
    Alternate Lp: 22 µH pot-core inductor (much easier to wind and trim).

  F02 — 80M (3.75 MHz, BW 500 kHz, Q=7.5):
    Lp = 5.6 µH (T50-6, 38 turns 24 AWG)
    Cp = 330 pF (NP0)
    Cs = 100 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~0.8 dB

  F03 — 40M (7.15 MHz, BW 300 kHz, Q=23.8):
    Lp = 2.2 µH (T50-6, 24 turns 24 AWG)
    Cp = 220 pF (NP0)
    Cs = 33 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~0.7 dB

  F04 — 30M (10.125 MHz, BW 50 kHz, Q=202) — 5-pole for narrower BW:
    5-pole needed for high Q. Practical 3-pole has minimum BW ~250 kHz.
    PRACTICAL COMPROMISE: Use 3-pole with 250 kHz BW (centered on 10.125 MHz).
    Lp = 1.8 µH (T50-6, 22 turns)
    Cp = 140 pF (100 + 39 pF NP0)
    Cs = 18 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~0.8 dB (accepts some adjacent-channel signals)
    NOTE: 30M band is narrow (50 kHz). No LC BPF can achieve 50 kHz BW at 10 MHz
    without complex crystal-filter construction. Use 250 kHz practical BW.

  F05 — 20M (14.175 MHz, BW 350 kHz, Q=40.5):
    Lp = 1.0 µH (T50-6, 17 turns)
    Cp = 120 pF NP0
    Cs = 22 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~0.7 dB

  F06 — 17M (18.118 MHz, BW 100 kHz, Q=181) — high Q:
    PRACTICAL BW: 200 kHz (achievable with precision components, VNA alignment)
    Lp = 680 nH (T50-6, 14 turns)
    Cp = 110 pF NP0 (82 + 27 pF)
    Cs = 12 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~1.0 dB (higher Q = higher loss)

  F07 — 15M (21.225 MHz, BW 450 kHz, Q=47.2):
    Lp = 560 nH (T50-6, 13 turns)
    Cp = 100 pF NP0
    Cs = 22 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~0.8 dB

  F08 — 12M (24.940 MHz, BW 100 kHz, Q=249) — use 200 kHz practical:
    Lp = 390 nH (T50-6, 11 turns)
    Cp = 100 pF NP0
    Cs = 10 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~1.2 dB

  F09 — 10M (28.85 MHz, BW 1700 kHz, Q=17):
    Lp = 330 nH (Coilcraft 0805CS-331 or wound, 10 turns T50-6)
    Cp = 82 pF NP0
    Cs = 56 pF NP0 (wider BW requires larger coupling cap)
    Passband insertion loss: ~0.6 dB

COMPONENT VALUES — VHF/UHF FILTERS
--------------------------------------
  VHF and UHF filters use helical resonator or interdigital topology.
  LC lumped elements practical to ~200 MHz; above that use distributed.

  F10 — 6M (52 MHz, BW 4 MHz, Q=13):
    LUMPED LC (still practical at 52 MHz):
    Lp = 100 nH (Coilcraft 0805HS-101, Q>30 at 50 MHz)
    Cp = 82 pF NP0
    Cs = 47 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~0.6 dB

  F11 — 2M (146 MHz, BW 4 MHz, Q=36.5):
    HELICAL RESONATOR DESIGN:
    3 helical resonators in cavity.
    Each resonator: 10 mm diameter, 30 mm length, 8T winding.
    Cavity: aluminum, 70 × 25 × 25 mm.
    Coupling: inductive loop at input/output (2T link coil).
    Tuning: adjust screws to compress/expand coil.
    Expected insertion loss: 2–4 dB.

    ALTERNATIVE — LUMPED LC (easier to build, more loss):
    Lp = 22 nH (Coilcraft 0805HS-22NK, SRF > 500 MHz)
    Cp = 47 pF NP0 (verify resonance: f0 = 1/(2π√(LC)) ≈ 157 MHz with 22nH/47pF)
    Cs = 6.8 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~2 dB (lumped at 146 MHz has higher loss)

  F12 — 1.25M (223.5 MHz, BW 3 MHz):
    INTERDIGITAL BPF or LUMPED:
    Lp = 12 nH, Cp = 39 pF → f0 = 232 MHz (adjust for 223 MHz)
    Cs = 4.7 pF NP0
    Passband insertion loss: ~2.5 dB (lumped, acceptable)

  F13 — 70cm (435 MHz, BW 30 MHz):
    STRIPLINE or MICROSTRIP BPF (PCB-based):
    3 coupled half-wavelength resonators on 1.6mm FR4.
    λ/2 at 435 MHz ≈ 34.5 mm (effective in FR4: 34.5/√4.4 = 16.4 mm)
    Coupling gap: 0.2–0.5 mm (tuned for BW)
    PCB width for 50Ω: ~3 mm on 1.6 mm FR4
    Insertion loss: 2–3 dB on FR4 (higher loss substrate than PTFE)
    For < 1.5 dB loss: use Rogers RO4003C substrate.

    PURCHASED ALTERNATIVE: 70cm BPF module (Minicircuits SBP-435 or similar) ~$20.

  F14 — 33cm (915 MHz, BW 26 MHz):
    SAW FILTER (simplest for UHF): Murata SAFEA915MHZ0F00R0 or similar.
    SAW (915 MHz ISM band): insertion loss 1.5–3 dB, shape factor excellent.
    Caution: SAW has fixed frequency, not tunable.
    Wiring: input match (39 pF to GND) ── SAW ── output match (39 pF to GND).

    STRIPLINE ALTERNATIVE: same as 70cm but scaled for 915 MHz.
    λ/2 in FR4 at 915 MHz: 7.8 mm (very small, tight tolerance needed).

  F15 — 23cm/20cm (1270 MHz, BW 60 MHz):
    SAW FILTER (1.27 GHz GPS/ham band): Murata SAFFB1G27FA0F0A.
    Or: cavity filter (SMA in/out), small aluminum machined cavity.
    Insertion loss SAW: 2–4 dB.
    PCB coupling lines: λ/2 at 1270 MHz ≈ 5.6 mm (requires precision PCB).

  F16 — BYPASS (wideband):
    Relay K_bypass closes SMA-to-SMA path; no filtering.
    Useful when wideband spectrum analysis desired (panadapter).

RELAY SWITCHING ARCHITECTURE
------------------------------
  16 filter positions require 16 relay outputs.
  Two layers of SP4T switching:

  Layer 1 (Band Group Select): 1× SP4T = selects HF-Low, HF-High, VHF, UHF
    K_GRP1 (DPDT/SP4T relay or 4× SPST relays):
      Position 1: Route to HF-Low BPF bank (F01–F04)
      Position 2: Route to HF-High BPF bank (F05–F09)
      Position 3: Route to VHF BPF bank (F10–F12)
      Position 4: Route to UHF BPF bank (F13–F15) + Bypass (F16)

  Layer 2 (Individual Band Select): 4× SP4T = selects within each group
    K_HF_LO (routes to F01, F02, F03, or F04)
    K_HF_HI (routes to F05, F06, F07, F08, or F09)  ← needs SP5T; use SP4T + relay
    K_VHF   (routes to F10, F11, or F12)
    K_UHF   (routes to F13, F14, F15, or F16 bypass)

  RELAY TYPES:
    HF paths (< 100 MHz): use Axicom V23105 SPDT 5V coil, RF-rated.
    VHF paths (< 500 MHz): use Panasonic TQ2-5V or equivalent RF relay.
    UHF paths (> 500 MHz): use high-frequency relay (Pickering 50-series) or
      PIN diode switch (PE4259 or HMC544A GaAs SPDT, 0.1–4 GHz).

  CONTROL LINES:
    74HC154 (4-to-16 line decoder, active-low outputs):
      Inputs: A0–A3 from ESP32 GPIO[3:0]
      Outputs Y0–Y15: each low output → ULN2003A → relay coil
      Power: 5V for relay coils, 3.3V for 74HC154 inputs

    ESP32 GPIO pin map:
      GPIO26: BPF_A0 (LSB of band select)
      GPIO27: BPF_A1
      GPIO14: BPF_A2
      GPIO12: BPF_A3 (MSB)
      GPIO33: BPF_EN  (enable decoder, active HIGH)
      Total: 5 GPIO for full 16-band selection

ULN2003A RELAY DRIVER WIRING
------------------------------
  74HC154 output Y_n (active LOW, 0V when selected) →
  ULN2003A INn (active HIGH input, needs inversion) →
  NEED INVERTER: 74HC04 inverter between 74HC154 and ULN2003A.

  OR: use 74HC138 (active LOW outputs) + ULN2003A (active HIGH input):
  Actually ULN2003A drives relay when input is HIGH (sinks current from +5V).
  74HC154 output = LOW when active.
  So: 74HC154 Y_n ── 74HC04 inv ── ULN2003A IN_n ── relay coil GND side.
      Relay coil (+5V) ──────────────────────────── relay coil power side.

  Wiring per relay:
    +5V ──────── Relay coil pin 1
    ULN2003A OUT ─── Relay coil pin 2
    ULN2003A IN  ─── 74HC04 output
    74HC04 IN    ─── 74HC154 Y_n

  Flyback diode: ULN2003A has internal flyback diodes (1N4148 equivalent).
  External 1N4148 across relay coil also acceptable for extra protection.

FILTER BOARD LAYOUT GUIDELINES
---------------------------------
  PCB: 160 × 100 mm, 2-layer, 1 oz copper, FR4.
  Ground plane: solid on bottom layer.
  All GND connections: via to bottom ground plane.

  Filter sections: physical grouping by band.
    Section A (HF-Low: 160m, 80m, 40m, 30m): left 40mm of board.
    Section B (HF-High: 20m–10m): center-left 40mm.
    Section C (VHF: 6m, 2m, 1.25m): center-right.
    Section D (UHF: 70cm–23cm + bypass): right.
    Relay bank: along bottom edge.
    74HC154 + ULN2003A + 74HC04: near right edge.

  Trace width for 50Ω:
    HF sections (<30 MHz): width not critical (<10% effect on HF filter)
    VHF sections (>30 MHz): 3.0 mm for 50Ω on 1.6mm FR4
    UHF sections (>300 MHz): 3.0 mm or use PTFE/Rogers substrate
