RESISTOR MOUNTING DETAIL:
  10x Ohmite RH series or equivalent, 500R 50W ceramic body, axial leads

  Bottom 5 (R1-R5):  Leads bent and soldered to center stud and ground ring
  Top 5 (R6-R10):    Same but inverted, also to center stud (extended) and ring

  This "double-decker" arrangement minimizes lead length and inductance.
  All resistors fully submerged in oil.

  CRITICAL: Resistor body must NOT touch can wall. Maintain 5mm clearance.
            Resistors must be fully immersed in oil (oil level above top bank).
            Do not operate if oil level falls below top bank.


CONNECTING STUD:
  Brass rod, 1/4"-20 thread, 200mm long
  Connected to N-jack center pin with silver solder
  All R hot ends soldered to stud at appropriate heights
  Stud isolated from can lid by PTFE standoff


GROUND RING (per bank):
  Copper ring, OD = 120mm, 3mm wide x 3mm thick
  All R cold ends soldered to ring
  Ring soldered to can interior bottom for lower bank
  Ring soldered to can wall via copper strap for upper bank

GROUND STRAP:
  Copper braid, 10mm x 2mm, from upper ring to N-jack shell
  Keep as short as possible (<30mm)


OIL FILL PROCEDURE:
  1. Complete all internal wiring and verify 50 ohm DC resistance.
  2. Clean interior with isopropyl alcohol. Dry completely (heat gun).
  3. Fill with naphthenic mineral oil (transformer oil or food-grade)
     until oil is 15mm above top resistor bank.
  4. Install pressure relief plug (1/8" NPT brass fitting with 0.5mm orifice
     or loose cap that vents when oil boils/expands).
  5. Do NOT seal completely. Oil expands ~7% from 20C to 100C.
     A sealed airtight container will build pressure.

OIL SPECIFICATIONS:
  Type:         Naphthenic mineral oil, Type II transformer oil
  Alternatives: Pure mineral oil (food-grade), Mobil Diala S4 ZX-I
  Avoid:        Motor oil (sulfur compounds degrade insulation)
                Synthetic oil (flammable at high temperatures)
                Water or glycol (corrosion, low dielectric strength)
  Dielectric:   >30 kV/mm (new oil)
  Flash point:  >130C (mineral oil)

  Volume required: 3.5 liters (1-gallon can size)
                   1.5 liters (1-quart can size, 200W version)


TEMPERATURE MONITORING:
  NTC thermistor, 10k @ 25C, stainless steel probe, 150mm insertion depth
  Inserted through 1/4"-28 drilled/tapped hole in can lid
  Sealed with PTFE tape on threads
  Connected to CYD monitor or dedicated meter

  Temperature limits:
    Normal operation:  <80C oil temperature
    Warning threshold: 85C  (LED/alarm)
    Shutdown:          95C  (relay opens antenna switch)
    Oil flash point:   130C (mineral oil) -- DO NOT APPROACH


PERFORMANCE SPECIFICATIONS
