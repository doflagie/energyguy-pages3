VOLTAGE/CURRENT LEVELS AT 1kW:
  V_rms  = sqrt(1000 x 50) = 223.6 Vrms
  V_peak = 316 V peak
  I_rms  = sqrt(1000/50) = 4.47 Arms through load
  Current per resistor = 4.47/20 = 0.224 A  (in 1000R: P = 0.224^2 x 1000 = 50W)

DIELECTRIC STRESS:
  316V peak on center stud to can wall (approx 50mm clearance)
  Electric field: 316V / 50mm = 6.3 kV/m
  Mineral oil dielectric: 30,000 kV/m (>>6.3 kV/m) -- ADEQUATE MARGIN


DIRECTIONAL COUPLER (integrated):
  Resistive bridge coupler, 20 dB, 1 MHz - 500 MHz
  (See sch_dir_coupler.txt for full schematic)

  Forward power port: SMA female, panel mount on enclosure side
  Reflected power port: SMA female, panel mount on enclosure side
  Coupler board: 50mm x 30mm, FR4, mounted inside enclosure lid

THERMAL MODEL:
  At 1kW continuous:
    Oil temp rise: dT/dt = P / (m_oil x Cp_oil)
                         = 1000 / (4.5 x 0.86 x 1800)
                         = 1000 / 6966
                         = 0.144 C/second

    Time to 80C limit from 25C ambient:
      t = (80 - 25) / 0.144 = 382 seconds = 6.4 minutes

  At 1kW with natural radiation from aluminum walls:
    Wall area: 2x(150x300) + 2x(150x300) + 150x150 = ~0.23 m^2
    Radiation: ~15W/C above ambient for polished aluminum
    Steady state: T_ss = 25 + 1000/15 = 92C (exceeds limit)

  CONCLUSION: 1kW requires either:
    (a) Duty cycle <= 30% (10 min on, 23 min off)
    (b) External forced-air cooling across aluminum walls
    (c) Oil circulation pump (aquarium pump, 5 L/min adequate)

  Recommended: Operate 1kW with oil pump circulation + external radiator.
               At 500W continuous, no cooling required.
               At 1kW: 10-minute maximum continuous.


PROTECTION CIRCUITS:
  Over-temperature relay:
    K1: DPDT relay, 12VDC coil, NC contacts in antenna switch line
    TH1: 10k NTC in oil, monitored by comparator circuit or CYD
    At T > 95C: relay opens, disconnects TX from load
    Red LED indicator for fault state

  Power indicator:
    Green LED via 10k in series to indicator port on coupler board
    Illuminates when RF present (detected by forward power detector)
