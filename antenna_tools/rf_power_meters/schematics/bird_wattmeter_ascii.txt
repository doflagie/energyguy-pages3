  BIRD-TYPE THRULINE DIRECTIONAL WATTMETER  —  TM-PWR-BIRD-001 Rev A
  Thruline body + interchangeable plug-in frequency/power elements
  Covers HF/VHF/UHF with separate slugs per band/range

OPERATING PRINCIPLE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  The Bird-type wattmeter uses a "thruline" body: a section of 50Ω coaxial
  line containing a transverse slot into which a plug-in "slug" element is
  inserted.  The slug contains a small directional coupler (loop or capacitive)
  that samples either the forward OR the reflected wave, depending on the slug
  orientation.  Rotating the slug 180° switches between FWD and REF readings.

  The thruline body maintains near-perfect 50Ω continuity whether or not a
  slug is inserted.  Without a slug, it is a transparent in-line connector.

SLUG INTERNAL SCHEMATIC — FORWARD POWER DETECTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  The slug contains a directional coupler using a short coupling section.
  At HF: small ferrite bead or air-core loop
  At VHF: coupled microstrip card (PCB wafer inside slug)
  At UHF: capacitive coupling tab + microstrip

  COUPLING ELEMENT:

  Center conductor ──[LOOP L1, ~1T]──┬────────────────────
                                      │
                                 COUPLING
                                 WINDING (N2 turns)
                                      │
                              ┌───────┴────────┐
                              │                │
                           R_term             ►D1 SCHOTTKY
                           50Ω               1N5711 / HSMS
                              │                │
                              │           C_filter 10nF
                             GND               │
                                          R_load 10kΩ
                                               │
                                     METER OUTPUT (+)
                                               │
                                        (GND at meter jack)


  SLUG ORIENTATION:
    Slug inserted normal:  FWD coupling  → reads forward power
    Slug rotated 180°:     REF coupling  → reads reflected power
    SWR computed from two readings.

METER CIRCUIT  (in body, driven by slug output)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  SLUG     R_protect            METER
  OUTPUT ──[100Ω]──┬────────────┤M1 200µA FSD├──── GND
                   │
                  Z1 TVS 6.2V   (overload clamp)
                   │
                  GND

  Analog meter face provides two scales:
    — Watts scale  (calibrated for slug range, nonlinear due to V² law)
    — SWR scale    (% reflected overlay, labeled 1.0 – ∞)

  OPTIONAL DIGITAL INTERFACE:
    — ADS1115 samples slug output voltage (0–200 mV)
    — ESP32 computes power using calibration table
    — OLED or CYD display
    — BT/USB log output


METER BODY DIMENSIONS  (Bird Model 43 compatible)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Body:
    OD: 50mm  ×  155mm length
    Slug socket: 25.4mm (1") diameter cylinder, centered, transverse
    Connector: SO-239 both ends (or N-type for VHF/UHF version)
    Meter window: 70mm × 30mm on top face
    Retention spring for slug (radial O-ring groove)

  Slug:
    OD: 25.0mm  ×  25.4mm length
    Flat on one side (orientation key, asymmetric for FWD/REF)
    Banana jack: 2mm, bottom center, contacts body spring contact
    PCB inside: 22mm × 20mm wafer with coupler and detector
