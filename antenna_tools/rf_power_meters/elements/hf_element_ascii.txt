ELEMENT MECHANICAL SPECIFICATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Slug body:   25.0mm OD × 25.4mm length (1.000" × 1.000")
  Material:    6061-T6 aluminum, black anodized
  Orientation: Flat on one side (D-shape cross-section) — prevents 90° errors
  Contact:     2mm banana pin, center bottom, gold-plated
  PCB:         22mm × 18mm, 1.6mm FR4, contacts body spring pin
  Retention:   O-ring groove at body mid-point; O-ring: 25mm × 2mm Buna-N

  Flatted side orientation:
    — Flat toward meter face = FORWARD power reading
    — Flat away from meter face = REFLECTED power reading
  (Same convention as Bird Model 43)

  PCB LAYOUT (22mm × 18mm):

    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  C  O  U  P  L  E  R    S  E  C  T  I  O  N  (left half)         │
    │  [TOROID PADS]  [R1 51Ω]  [R2 51Ω]                                │
    │─────────────────────────────────────────│
    │  D  E  T  E  C  T  O  R    S  E  C  T  I  O  N  (right half)     │
    │  [D1 1N5711]  [C_hold]  [R_load]  [J_out banana]                  │
    └──────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Polarity:  Forward: D1 anode → SEC(+)  (as drawn above)
             Reflected: rotate slug 180° → D1 anode connects to SEC(-) instead


SECONDARY WINDING DATA BY RANGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Element   Range      N2 Turns  Coupling(dB)  V_out FS   Scale Factor
  ────────  ─────────  ────────  ────────────  ─────────  ────────────
  HF-10     1–10W      32T       –30.1 dB      141 mV     1.00 (ref)
  HF-100    10–100W    10T       –20.0 dB       45 mV     0.316
  HF-1K     100–1kW   100T       –40.0 dB       45 mV     0.100
  HF-3K     1kW–3kW   178T       –45.0 dB       40 mV     0.056

  Full-scale voltage calculation (at P_max, Z0=50Ω):
    V_rf   = √(P × 2 × 50) = √(100 × P) [V rms]
    V_coup = V_rf × 10^(C_dB/20)  [V rms at secondary]
    V_det  = V_coup × 0.9          [≈ diode rectified, square-law]
    V_out  ≈ V_coup  (in square-law region)

  For HF-10 at 10W:
    V_rf  = √(10 × 100) = 31.6 V rms
    V_coup= 31.6 × 10^(–30/20) = 31.6 × 0.0316 = 1.00 V
    V_det ≈ 1.00² / (2 × 50 × R_load/1000)  [square-law: V_out ∝ P]
    Simplified: V_out ≈ 0.141 V at 10W  (141 mV FS)

  Meter scales:
    Meter FS = 200 µA.  Series resistor R3 = 141 mV / 200 µA = 705Ω → use 680Ω.
    For HF-100: R3 = 45 mV / 200 µA = 225Ω → use 220Ω.


WINDING INSTRUCTIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Core:  Fair-Rite 5943000401 (Mix #43, single-hole, OD=9.5mm, ID=4.8mm)
         Inductance: Al = 523 nH/T²

  Primary:
    (1) Cut one piece of #16 AWG solid copper, 80mm long.
    (2) Pass straight through core bore once.
    (3) This is the 1-turn primary — it IS the center conductor of the slug body.

  Secondary (example: 32 turns for HF-10):
    (1) Cut 450mm of #30 AWG enameled magnet wire.
    (2) Mark one end with red paint = START (+ terminal).
    (3) Wind 32 turns evenly around core.  Wind left-to-right.
    (4) Solder START end to PAD_SEC_PLUS on PCB.
    (5) Solder END (bare) to PAD_SEC_MINUS.
    (6) Verify: winding direction relative to primary determines polarity.
        If slug reads REF when it should read FWD, reverse secondary leads.

  Inductance check (use LCR meter at 1 kHz):
    L_secondary = Al × N2² = 523 nH/T² × 32² = 535 µH  (HF-10 element)
    L must be > 10× the secondary termination reactance at lowest frequency:
    X_L at 1.8 MHz = 2π × 1.8e6 × 535e-6 = 6.05 kΩ  >> 51Ω ✓

  CAUTION:  Below approximately 100 kHz the inductance may be insufficient
            to terminate the secondary properly.  Minimum recommended
            operating frequency for HF-10 element is 1.8 MHz.


TEMPERATURE COMPENSATION (HF elements)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Option A (passive, no firmware needed):
    Add D2 (same type as D1, 1N5711) in the output circuit as compensation.
    D2 is mounted thermally coupled to D1 (back-to-back on PCB, same copper pour).
    D2 is RF-shielded by surrounding it with ground copper pour.

          VPEAK_DET ──[R_series 100Ω]──┬──► VOUT (to meter)
                                        │
                                      ──►── D2 (compensation, no RF input)
                                        │
                                       GND

    D2 generates a temperature-dependent forward voltage that partially
    cancels D1's temperature drift.  Residual error: ~±3% over ±25°C.

  Option B (firmware, digital element with ADC output):
    NTC thermistor glued to D1 on slug PCB, output on second banana pin.
    Body reads both V_det and V_ntc, applies correction.
    See detector_circuits_ascii.txt, Method B for firmware algorithm.
