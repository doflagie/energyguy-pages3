SCHEMATIC: 4-WAY WILKINSON POWER DIVIDER
TM-HYB-001  Rev A
Two-stage binary tree, equal-split, in-phase, resistive isolation
50Ω system, covering 40M through 23cm
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
A 4-way equal-split divider constructed from three 2-way Wilkinson stages
arranged in a binary tree.  All outputs are in-phase (0° relative).
Total insertion loss: 6.02 dB (3 dB × 2 stages).

Topology:

  PORT 1 (input) ─── [Stage A: 2-way] ─┬─── [Stage B: 2-way] ─── PORT 2
                                        │                       └─── PORT 3
                                        └─── [Stage C: 2-way] ─── PORT 4
                                                                └─── PORT 5

Stage A: Z0 = 50Ω input; two λ/4 arms of 70.7Ω; R_iso = 100Ω.
Stages B, C: Z0 = 50Ω input (from Stage A output); same as Stage A.

NOTE: Intermediate junction impedance = 50Ω throughout.
The junction between Stage A output and Stage B/C input is 50Ω.
No impedance transformation needed at junctions.

NON-EQUAL SPLIT WILKINSON (Power Division Ratios)
---------------------------------------------------
For unequal division (port 2: port 3 = k²:1):

  Z_arm_2 = Z0 × √(1+k²) / k      (arm to high-power output)
  Z_arm_3 = Z0 × √(1+k²) × k      (arm to low-power output)
  R_iso    = Z0 × (k + 1/k)

  Common ratios:
  Split ratio   k      Z_arm_2 (Ω)  Z_arm_3 (Ω)  R_iso (Ω)
  -----------   ---    -----------  -----------  ---------
  50/50         1.0    70.7         70.7         100
  75/25         1.73   58.1         100.0        144
  90/10         3.0    52.7         158.1        317

  These asymmetric dividers are useful for power monitoring (tap 10% for
  measurement, pass 90% to antenna).


8-WAY DIVIDER (3 STAGES)
--------------------------
Add a third stage of 2-way Wilkinson after each of the 4 outputs.
  Total components: 7 isolation resistors, 7 × 2 inductor sets.
  Insertion loss: 9.03 dB.
  Maximum bandwidth: same as single stage (≈ 2:1).

For wider bandwidth in multi-way dividers, use lossless reactive splitter
(Gysel divider) or transmission-line tree with grounded resistors.

LAYOUT NOTES
-------------
  HF (through 30 MHz): Keep all component leads < 5 mm.
  Avoid ground loops between stages; use star ground at center.
  Shield stages B and C if inter-stage crosstalk is a problem.
  For outdoor use, pot finished board in urethane conformal coat.
