  Dual-Stage: Differential + Common-Mode Filtering, MOV Transient Protection
  Document: TM-RFI-SCH-001 Rev A

OVERVIEW
--------
  This filter installs at the AC power entry point of the ham shack.
  It attenuates both conducted interference injected FROM the shack into the
  mains (harmonic suppression from transmitters) and interference arriving
  FROM the mains to the shack equipment (plasma TVs, switching supplies, etc.).

  Two independent noise paths:
  1. Differential-mode (DM): noise between Line and Neutral
     → suppressed by X-capacitors and series inductors
  2. Common-mode (CM): noise on Line+Neutral together relative to Earth Ground
     → suppressed by Y-capacitors and common-mode choke

KEY SPECIFICATIONS
------------------
  Voltage:          120V AC or 240V AC (component selection per market)
  Current:          20A maximum (use 10A fuse for typical shack loads)
  Frequency range:  10 kHz – 30 MHz attenuation (covers HF interference)
  DM attenuation:   >40 dB at 1 MHz, >60 dB at 10 MHz
  CM attenuation:   >50 dB at 1 MHz, >70 dB at 10 MHz
  Transient:        MOV clamps at 275V (120V line) or 470V (240V line)
  Enclosure:        PETG 3D printed, 180×90×60mm

SAFETY WARNING
--------------
  THIS CIRCUIT OPERATES AT LETHAL MAINS VOLTAGE (120V/240V AC).
  Construction must comply with applicable electrical codes.
  All mains-voltage components must be rated for mains use (X2/Y2 capacitors).
  Use only UL/CE listed fuse holders and circuit breakers.
  Install in grounded metal or heavy-walled non-metallic enclosure.
  All mains wiring must use properly rated wire (14 AWG minimum for 15A circuits).
  DO NOT use in wet locations. Do not service while energized.

SCHEMATIC — COMPLETE MAINS EMI FILTER
----------------------------------------

  MAINS IN:                                         MAINS OUT:
  L (Hot/Line)  ─────────────────────────────────  L' (to shack)
  N (Neutral)   ─────────────────────────────────  N' (to shack)
  E (Earth GND) ─────────────────────────────────  E' (to shack)

  DETAILED PATH:

  L ──── F1 (fuse) ──── node L1 ──── L_CM_a ──── node L2 ──── X1 ──── node L3 ──── L'
                                │                  │                  │
                              MOV1              CX1 (0.1µF)        CX2 (0.1µF)
                                │                  │                  │
  N ──────────────── node N1 ──── L_CM_b ──── node N2 ──── X2 ──── node N3 ──── N'
                       │                  │
                       CY1 (2.2nF)       CY2 (2.2nF)
                       │                  │
  E ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── E'

COMPONENT DETAIL
-----------------

  F1: Fuse, 10A slow-blow, 250V (120V) or 250V (240V)
    Holder: panel-mount fuse holder, IEC 5×20mm
    Location: Line side, immediately at input terminal
    Purpose: overcurrent protection

  MOV1: Metal Oxide Varistor
    120V mains: V275LA10A (Littelfuse) — clamp at 275V RMS
    240V mains: V470LA40A (Littelfuse) — clamp at 470V RMS
    Energy rating: 10J minimum (LA40A = 40J for higher energy surges)
    Connection: Line to Neutral (across the line)
    Additional MOVs: L-to-Ground and N-to-Ground (3-element protection)

  L_CM: Common-Mode Choke (CM inductor, bifilar wound)
    Core: FT-240-31 or FT-240-43 ferrite toroid, 2.4" OD
    Winding: 11 turns bifilar (both conductors wound together)
    Wire: 16 AWG insulated hookup wire (not magnet wire — mains isolation)
    Inductance: ~1.2 mH per winding (mutual = nearly total inductance)
    CM impedance at 1 MHz: 7.5 kΩ (XL = 2π × 1e6 × 1.2e-3)
    DM inductance: near zero (bifilar flux cancels for DM)

    WINDING:
    - Tape or wrap FT-240-31 with 3 layers Kapton tape (mains isolation)
    - Cut 1m of 16AWG black and 1m of 16AWG white (or stripe)
    - Thread both wires together through core 11 times
    - Black = Line; White = Neutral
    - Mark start/finish to identify winding polarity
    - Test CM choke with LCR meter: short one coil, measure other = near zero (DM); open one, measure other = L_CM

  CX1, CX2: X2-rated capacitors (across-the-line, differential)
    Value: 0.1 µF (100 nF) X2, 275V AC X2 rated
    Manufacturer examples: Kemet R463I310000M0K, WIMA MKP-X2-0.1µF
    CAUTION: Must be X2 rated. Standard capacitors will fail under mains voltage surge.
    Purpose: DM filter, shunts high-frequency differential noise

  CY1, CY2: Y2-rated capacitors (line-to-ground, common-mode)
    Value: 2.2 nF (2200 pF) Y2, 250V AC Y2 rated
    Note: Y-caps connect to Earth Ground — leakage current consideration.
      Max leakage per IEC: 3.5 mA. With 2× 2.2nF at 120V/60Hz:
      I_leak = 120 × 2π × 60 × 2.2e-9 = 0.1 mA per cap (well within limit)
    Manufacturer: Vishay VY1222M31Y5US63V0, Kemet S series
    CAUTION: Must be Y2 rated. Y2 = fail-short mode is safe (fuse clears).

  X1, X2: Second-stage X caps (optional, for double-stage filter):
    Same specification as CX1, CX2.
    If single-stage filter is adequate, omit X1/X2 and connect L2-L3 directly.

PCB LAYOUT GUIDELINES
----------------------
  - All mains-side components: 8 mm minimum creepage/clearance to each other
  - Safety ground bus: heavy copper trace (at least 2mm wide) to earth terminal
  - Y-capacitors: physically close to ground terminal
  - CM choke: central location, equal lead lengths to Line/Neutral
  - MOV: near input terminals
  - Fuse: first component on Line conductor from input
  - Use terminal blocks rated ≥300V AC, ≥15A for mains connections
  - Separate mains-side from load-side with 5mm minimum clearance on PCB

ADDITIONAL FILTER STAGES (OPTIONAL)
--------------------------------------
  For extremely high noise environments, add SECOND stage:

  After CX1/CX2, add another L-C stage:
  L' ─── Lseries (1µH) ─── Cx_out (0.1µF) ─── L''
  N' ─── Lseries (1µH) ─── Cx_out (0.1µF) ─── N''

  Series inductors: 1–5 µH, rated for line current (10A)
  Use: Coilcraft IHD series, toroidal power inductors
  This adds ~30 dB additional DM attenuation at 1 MHz.

INSTALLATION — POWER DISTRIBUTION BOX
----------------------------------------
  Install filter in a metal power distribution box:

  1. Mount in metal box (NEMA 1 or 2, grounded)
  2. Earth ground: #12 AWG green to earth ground of building
  3. Input: 3-conductor 14AWG to mains outlet or breaker panel
  4. Output: IEC C13 sockets (×6) for shack equipment
  5. Label panel: "SHACK EMI FILTER — MAINS VOLTAGE"
  6. Circuit breaker: 15A in-line (SQUARE D QO115 or equiv.) for overcurrent
