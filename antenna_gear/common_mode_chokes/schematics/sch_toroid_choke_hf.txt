SCHEMATIC: Toroidal Common-Mode Choke — HF Bands 160M through 10M
TM-CMC-001  Rev A
FT-240 and FT-140 Ferrite Toroid Wound Coaxial Chokes
Covers: 1.8 MHz (160M) through 28 MHz (10M) and 50 MHz (6M)
================================================================================

THEORY OF OPERATION
───────────────────
A common-mode choke is wound by passing coaxial cable (or twisted wire pair)
through a ferrite toroid core.  The differential mode (transmit/receive signal
traveling on the inner conductor relative to the outer shield) sees essentially
zero impedance from the core — both conductors thread through equally, fields
cancel.

The common-mode current (current flowing on the OUTSIDE of the coax shield,
seeking ground via feedline) sees full choking impedance.  The core presents
a series impedance Z_choke to the common-mode path.

Common-mode rejection in dB:
  CMR = 20 × log₁₀( 1 + Z_choke / Z_load )
  where Z_load is the impedance of the path common-mode current wants to take
  (typically 50–300 Ω for antenna/station ground path)

For Z_choke = 1000 Ω, Z_load = 100 Ω:
  CMR = 20 × log₁₀(1 + 10) = 20.8 dB  (significant but not extreme)

For Z_choke = 5000 Ω, Z_load = 100 Ω:
  CMR = 20 × log₁₀(1 + 50) = 34.1 dB  (excellent RFI suppression)

Minimum useful Z_choke for antenna feedpoint balun: ≥ 1000 Ω
Good practice target: ≥ 2000 Ω across entire operating frequency range
Excellent: ≥ 5000 Ω (type 31 material, multi-core stacked)

DESIGN 1: FT-240-31 BROADBAND HF CHOKE (160M–15M)
================================================================================

Core: FT-240-31 (Mix 31), 61mm OD, 35mm ID, 12.7mm height
Cable: RG-8X (8.4mm OD), or RG-58 (5mm OD)
Turns: 10–12

WINDING PROCEDURE:
  10 turns RG-8X on FT-240-31:
  1. Cut 2.2m of RG-8X.
  2. Mark center of core with tape.
  3. Pass coax through core hole; leave 30cm tail for connector.
  4. Wind 10 turns in same direction (CW looking at core face).
     Space turns evenly; occupy ~300° of core circumference.
     Final gap ~60° between first and last turn (avoid exceeding 320°).
  5. Leave 30cm tail for second connector.
  6. Seal core in weatherproof enclosure (see OpenSCAD design).

TURNS COUNT vs. PERFORMANCE (FT-240-31, RG-8X):

  Turns | Z @3.5 MHz | Z @14 MHz | Max power (CW) | Core temp rise
  ------|-----------|----------|----------------|---------------
    8   |  2100 Ω   |  2750 Ω  |   1500W        | <10°C @ 100W
   10   |  3200 Ω   |  4250 Ω  |   1500W        | <12°C @ 100W
   12   |  4500 Ω   |  5900 Ω  |   1000W        | <15°C @ 100W
   14   |  5800 Ω   |  7200 Ω  |   600W         | ~20°C @ 100W
  Note: More turns → higher common-mode impedance but also slightly more
  differential loss and greater inter-winding capacitance (limits upper freq).

RECOMMENDED TURNS BY OPERATING BAND:

  Operating band(s) | Core    | Turns | Coax       | Comment
  ------------------|---------|-------|------------|---------------------------
  160M–80M (CW/DX)  | FT-240-31| 12–14| RG-8X     | Stack 2 cores for extra Z
  160M–40M (general)| FT-240-31| 10   | RG-8X     | Standard all-HF choke
  80M–20M           | FT-240-31|  8   | RG-58     | Compact, adequate for QRP
  40M–10M           | FT-240-43| 12   | RG-8X     | Upper HF preferred
  20M–6M            | FT-240-43|  8   | RG-8X     | Mix 43 advantage above 14 MHz

SCHEMATIC REPRESENTATION:

  COAX IN ──── CM CHOKE ─────────────────────── COAX OUT
             (inner coax)
             ══════════════════════════════════   ← signal passes, no change
             (outer shield - differential mode)

  Common-mode equivalent circuit:
                    Z_choke
  shield ──────────/\/\/\/\/──────────────── shield
  (CM path)     (ferrite toroid)      (CM path out)

  The toroid ONLY affects the common-mode (one-way) current on shield.
  Differential mode (signal between center conductor and shield) unaffected.

DESIGN 2: STACKED FT-240-31 + FT-240-43 WIDEBAND CHOKE (160M–10M)
================================================================================

Two cores stacked (concentric, wound as one unit) provide higher total impedance
and flatter response across 1.8–28 MHz.

Stack: FT-240-31 (bottom) + FT-240-43 (top), dimensions match
Cable: RG-8X, 8 turns through both stacked cores

INDIVIDUAL AND COMBINED IMPEDANCE:

  f (MHz) | Z_31 (8t) | Z_43 (8t) | Z_combined | Improvement
  --------|-----------|-----------|------------|------------
    1.8   |  1350 Ω   |   310 Ω   |  1660 Ω    | +23%
    3.5   |  2000 Ω   |   620 Ω   |  2620 Ω    | +31%
    7.0   |  2700 Ω   |  1980 Ω   |  4680 Ω    | +73%
   14.0   |  2750 Ω   |  3850 Ω   |  6600 Ω    | excellent
   21.0   |  2620 Ω   |  5220 Ω   |  7840 Ω    | excellent
   28.0   |  2320 Ω   |  5440 Ω   |  7760 Ω    | excellent

Stacking is the recommended approach for multi-band operation (contest station,
field day, Field operation QRP).  Two cores add only 27g and minimal bulk.

STACKING PROCEDURE:
  1. Align two cores face-to-face; tape together with self-amalgamating tape.
  2. Mark the resulting compound core as one unit.
  3. Wind 8 turns of RG-8X through BOTH cores simultaneously (treat as one core).
  4. Each pass of coax through both holes counts as 1 turn.

DESIGN 3: FT-140-43 COMPACT CHOKE (20M–10M, QRP/portable)
================================================================================

Core: FT-140-43 (Mix 43), 35.5mm OD, 23mm ID, 12.7mm height
Weight: 16g (very portable)
Cable: RG-58 or RG-174 (fits through 23mm ID easily)

WINDING (RG-58, 12 turns FT-140-43):
  Pass RG-58 through 12 times; total coax used ≈ 12 × (π × 0.03) = 1.1m
  Maximum turns limited by ID and cable diameter:
    RG-58 OD = 5mm; ID = 23mm; circumference = 72mm
    Maximum turns before cable won't fit: ~10–11 turns RG-58

PERFORMANCE FT-140-43, 10 turns RG-58:

  --------|--------
    7     |  1950
   14     |  3600
   21     |  4400
   28     |  4200
   50     |  2800

Excellent portable QRP choke for 40M–6M backpack operating.

================================================================================
POWER HANDLING
================================================================================

Ferrite core power handling is limited by:
1. Core temperature rise (Curie temp of Mix 31 = 230°C; Mix 43 = 200°C)
2. Coax voltage breakdown (not normally limiting at legal power levels)
3. SWR: high SWR concentrates voltage/current at specific points on feed line

At 100W CW, 10t FT-240-31, SWR = 1:1: temperature rise < 10°C (safe indefinitely)
At 100W CW, 10t FT-240-31, SWR = 3:1: temperature rise < 25°C (still safe)
At 1500W CW, 8t FT-240-31, SWR = 1:1: temperature rise < 50°C (safe in cool weather)
At 1500W CW, 8t FT-240-31, SWR = 3:1: temperature rise ≈ 80°C (monitor, limit key-down)

QRP operation (5W): no thermal concerns with any toroid configuration.
