SECTION 1 — CONCEPT
================================================================================

DESCRIPTION:
  A crank-up tower consists of nested tubular lattice sections elevated by a
  cable-and-winch or rack-and-pinion mechanism. Unlike push-up masts, a
  crank-up can support moderate antenna loads (5–15 kg) without guying in calm
  conditions, and heavy antenna loads when guyed. Lowering for maintenance
  and relocation does not require dismantling.

PORTABLE VERSION CONSTRAINTS:
  - All sections must break down to ≤2.0m for vehicle transport (fits in truck bed)
  - Total system weight including base: ≤75 kg (two-person lift)
  - Assembly time: ≤60 minutes for one experienced operator
  - Antenna load: up to 10 kg directional antenna (small 3-element Yagi, log-periodic)
  - Maximum operating height: 40 ft (12m)
  - Design wind speed: 70 mph (112 km/h) survival, 50 mph (80 km/h) operational

COMMON COMMERCIAL DESIGNS FOR REFERENCE:
  Rohn 25G / 45G crank-up: guyed lattice, field-proven, heavy
  US Tower TX-455 / TX-472: self-supporting for heavy beams; not truly portable
  Trylon T-600: lighter fiberglass lattice; suitable portable reference
  Hazer systems: motorized tram for installing antenna on fixed towers

SECTION 2 — STRUCTURAL DESIGN — PORTABLE 3-SECTION CRANK-UP
================================================================================

SECTION GEOMETRY:
  Each section is a triangular lattice (equilateral triangle cross-section):
  Face width: 250mm (10") per section — standard for 3-element Yagi loads
  Section length: 1.8m (6 ft) + 150mm overlap each end = 2.1m total per section

  Section | Position | Face Width | Chord OD | Bracing     | Weight
  --------|----------|------------|----------|-------------|-------
    A     | Bottom   | 250mm      | 25mm × 3 | 16mm tube   | 12 kg
    B     | Middle   | 200mm      | 22mm × 3 | 14mm tube   | 9 kg
    C     | Top      | 160mm      | 19mm × 3 | 12mm tube   | 7 kg

  All chord tubing: 6061-T6 aluminum
  All bracing: 6061-T6 aluminum tube or 6061-T6 angle

SECTION NESTING:
  Section C slides inside Section B; Section B slides inside Section A.
  Nesting clearance: 10mm face-to-face between nested sections.
  Locking: 10mm × 50mm locking bar and M8 bolt through chord at full extension.
  Alternatively: spring-loaded pawl engages ladder rung welded to chord.

CRANK MECHANISM:
  Option A — Cable/winch:
    1.5:1 mechanical advantage cable route over pulley at top of each section.
    Hand-crank winch at base (15:1 gear ratio → 22.5:1 total).
    Single cable runs through all sections (military push-up style).
    Cable size: 3/16" stainless 7×7; 1,200 lb breaking strength.

  Option B — Rack and pinion:
    Aluminum rack welded to one chord face.
    12-tooth 32-pitch spur gear on winch shaft → 24:1 gear ratio.
    More precise; heavier; better for permanent semi-portable installations.

  PREFERRED for field portable: Cable/winch (Option A)

CRANK WINCH SPECIFICATION:
  Ratio: 15:1 hand-crank gear
  Drum: 50mm diameter, 150mm wide (holds 6m of 3/16" cable)
  Brake: Automatic ratchet pawl (prevent unwinding under load)
  Handle: 250mm cranking radius
  Material: Galvanized steel or stainless; cast iron frame with bronze bushings
  Reference: Dutton-Lainson 1648A or equivalent (rated 900 lb line pull)

CABLE ROUTING:
                       ┌── Top Section (C)
                       │   [Pulley fixed to top of C]
                       │    ↑ cable from section B top
              ┌────────┘
              │ Middle Section (B)
              │   [Pulley fixed to top of B]
              │    ↑ cable from winch drum
    ┌─────────┘
    │ Bottom Section (A) — fixed to base
    │   [Winch drum at base of A]
    │   Hand crank at side of base section

  Cable attaches to top of Section C; routes down through pulleys; winds on drum.
  Cranking in: sections extend upward.
  Releasing brake + gravity: controlled descent.

SECTION 3 — BASE CONFIGURATION
================================================================================

PORTABLE BASE OPTIONS:

OPTION A — GROUND SPIKE / THRUST BEARING BASE:
  300mm × 60mm OD steel pipe driven 600mm into ground.
  Section A base slides over spike; lock pin through section A chord.
  Allows 360° rotation for antenna heading adjustment.
  Requires firm soil; not suitable for rocky/paved surfaces.

OPTION B — TRIPOD BASE:
  Three 2.4m × 50mm OD aluminum legs, spread to 1.5m radius.
  Each leg hinged at base collar; fold inward for transport.
  Level adjusters (threaded feet) at each leg tip.
  Center collar accepts Section A base (50mm bore).
  Base weight: 8 kg; supports up to 40 ft with 2-level guying.

OPTION C — VEHICLE HITCH MOUNT:
  2" receiver hitch adapter; base section clamps to receiver.
  Vehicle provides counterweight for lateral loads.
  Limits operating height to 25 ft (avoid contact with power lines)
  without additional guying; adequate for portable DX and satellite.
  See base_plates_mounts.scad: hitch_mast_mount()

OPTION D — BALLAST BASE PLATE:
  600mm × 600mm × 6mm steel plate; 180 kg sandbag ballast.
  Section A base plate bolts to steel plate with M12 bolts.
  Suitable for hard surfaces: parking lots, concrete pads.
  See base_plates_mounts.scad: ballast_base_plate()

SECTION 4 — CRANK-UP OPERATING PROCEDURES
================================================================================

ASSEMBLY (FROM TRANSPORT):
  1. Lay Section A on ground; attach tripod or base plate.
  2. Slide Section B into top of Section A; do not lock yet.
  3. Slide Section C into top of Section B; do not lock yet.
  4. Attach antenna to top of Section C at ground level.
  5. Route and attach crank cable through all pulleys; attach to Section C top.
  6. Erect tower (Section A) on base; secure base.
  7. Install guys at Section A top (Level 1) and Section B top (Level 2).
  8. Stake or anchor all guy wires; do not tension yet.

RAISING:
  1. With tower near vertical and base secured, begin cranking.
  2. Section B extends first until locked; continue cranking.
  3. Section C extends; antenna rises to operating height.
  4. Lock all section pins when fully extended.
  5. Tension guys Level 2 (upper) first, then Level 1.
  6. Final plumb check; adjust guys as needed.

LOWERING:
  1. Release guy tension Level 1, then Level 2.
  2. Release winch brake slowly — controlled descent only.
  3. NEVER release brake abruptly — free-fall will damage tower and antenna.
  4. When sections nested, insert transport lock pins.
  5. Disconnect guys and disassemble base for transport.

SAFE CRANKING SPEEDS:
  Raise: 1 revolution/2 seconds (comfortable sustained cranking)
  Lower: 1 revolution/3 seconds (brake friction assists control)
  Maximum allowed wind during crank operation: 25 mph (Beaufort 5)
  Do NOT crank above or below in wind >35 mph.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — ROTATOR INTEGRATION
================================================================================

TOP PLATE DESIGN:
  Section C top chord: weld or bolt a 200mm × 200mm × 6mm aluminum top plate.
  Top plate accepts:
    - Alliance HD rotator (U-bolt mounting, 50mm mast tube up through tower top)
    - Yaesu G-450A / G-800 (similar U-bolt mounting)
    - Ham-IV style (thrust bearing + torque arm)

  Mast tube up through rotator: 38mm OD × 2mm wall, 1m long
    Antenna attached to top of this tube.
    Rotator mounted at tower top plate; tube extends 600mm above plate.

ROTATOR CABLE ROUTING:
  Rotator cable runs inside Section C chord tube to base (hollow chord).
  Exit at Section A base through rubber grommet.
  Drip loop at tower base before entering controller.

CYD ROTATOR CONTROLLER:
  ESP32 + CYD at base of tower; reads compass heading via QMC5883L magnetometer.
  Display: bearing, target bearing, rotation speed, error from target.
  BLE interface to field laptop or smartphone for DX heading.
  See TM-MAST-001 CYD firmware reference.
