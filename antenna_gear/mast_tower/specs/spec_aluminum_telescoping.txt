SECTION 1 — OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Aluminum telescoping masts are the field standard for portable amateur radio
and military NVIS antenna support. Higher strength-to-weight ratio than
fiberglass; electrically conductive (must insulate if mast carries RF current);
less UV degradation; heavier per foot.

APPLICATIONS:
  - Mast-mounted Yagi or log-periodic for HF/VHF DX
  - NVIS dipole center support (insulate mast or use fiberglass extension at top)
  - Field expedient tower replacement for portable DX operation
  - Emergency communications infrastructure
  - Mast-mounted rotator with directional beam antenna

STANDARD MILITARY / COMMERCIAL MAST:
  MIL-DTL-3446A / AB-155/G series mast sections: 1.5" OD × 0.120" wall × 4 ft
  Commercial equivalent: Rohn HM push-up mast; Max-Gain Systems MG-LM mast
  Custom field-build: 6061-T6 tubing per this specification

SECTION 2 — MATERIAL SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

ALLOY:
  6061-T6 aluminum alloy
  Tensile strength:  310 MPa (45,000 psi)
  Yield strength:    276 MPa (40,000 psi)
  Elastic modulus:   68.9 GPa (10,000 ksi)
  Density:           2.70 g/cm³ (0.098 lb/in³)

SECTION SIZING (CUSTOM BUILD):
  Section | OD (mm) | Wall (mm) | Length  | Weight/m | Fits Inside
  --------|---------|-----------|---------|----------|-------------
    1 (top)|  38    |  2.0      |  1.5m   |  0.61 kg | Section 2
    2      |  44    |  2.5      |  1.5m   |  0.82 kg | Section 3
    3      |  50    |  2.5      |  1.5m   |  0.94 kg | Section 4
    4      |  57    |  3.0      |  1.5m   |  1.27 kg | Section 5
    5      |  63    |  3.0      |  1.5m   |  1.41 kg | Section 6
    6 (base)|  70   |  3.0      |  1.5m   |  1.57 kg | Base plate

  Total system weight (6 sections, 9m/30 ft): 8.3 kg bare mast

LOCKING PINS:
  Each section locked with a spring-loaded stainless pin (3mm × 20mm) through
  matching hole in lower section wall. 3mm drill through both walls; seat a
  compression spring behind the pin head. Or: hose clamp locking, same as
  fiberglass specification.

  PREFERRED: Welded locking ring (annular bead welded to lower section OD at
  maximum extension stop). Prevents over-extension without pin hardware.

SURFACE TREATMENT:
  Anodize (Type II clear) all sections after machining.
  Field units: Alodine 1200 (chromate conversion) for corrosion protection.
  Avoid bare aluminum — salt air and soil contact cause rapid pitting.

SECTION 3 — GUY WIRE SYSTEM
================================================================================

GUY WIRE MATERIAL:
  Primary:   3/16" (4.8mm) galvanized 7×19 aircraft cable
             Breaking strength: 4,100 lbs (1,860 kg)
             Stainless steel: 316 SS preferred for marine/coastal deployments

  Lightweight: 3mm Dyneema SK75 (synthetic)
             Breaking strength: ~1,800 kg; weight 17 g/m (80% lighter than steel)
             Use for portable SOTA/POTA where weight matters

  Avoid: Cheap braided steel (galvanized garden wire) — unreliable break strength
         Polypropylene rope — UV degrades within months; unpredictable stretch

GUY TERMINATION:
  Steel cable: Swaged or copper-crimped Nicopress thimble-and-loop
    - Use proper thimble in loop (prevents cable kinking at eye)
    - Minimum 3 Nicopress sleeves per termination
  Dyneema: Brummel splice (preferred) or double bowline knot
    - Do NOT use cable clamps (U-bolt type) on Dyneema

GUY ATTACHMENT TO MAST:
  Printed guy ring collar (see mast_collars.scad: guy_ring_collar())
  OR: Commercial stainless mast collar (Rohn AC20G, Coxswain style)
  OR: Welded lug on mast section at guying level

GUY PATTERN:
  3 guys per level at 120° spacing (standard)
  4 guys per level at 90° spacing (preferred in high-wind areas >50 mph design)

GUY ANCHOR RADIUS:
  Minimum: H × 0.5 (50% of mast height from base)
  Preferred: H × 0.7 (70% of mast height — optimum tension/force ratio)
  Maximum practical: H × 1.0 (100% — if space allows, reduces tension requirement)

  EXAMPLE: 30-foot (9m) mast:
    Minimum anchor radius: 4.5m from base
    Preferred anchor radius: 6.3m from base

GUY ANGLE FROM VERTICAL:
  At 50% radius: guy angle = arctan(0.5) = 26.6° from vertical (steep)
  At 70% radius: guy angle = arctan(0.7) = 35° from vertical (preferred)
  At 100% radius: guy angle = arctan(1.0) = 45° from vertical

  NOTE: Shallower angle (longer radius) reduces vertical compression load
  on mast but increases required guy wire length.

================================================================================
SECTION 4 — GUYING SCHEDULE
================================================================================

20-FOOT (6m) MAST:
  Level 1:  At 4.5m (15 ft)   — 3 guys, 70% radius = 3.15m from base
  Pre-tension each guy to 50–75 N (5–7.5 kgf)

30-FOOT (9m) MAST:
  Level 1:  At 3.6m (12 ft)   — 3 guys, 70% radius = 4.2m from base
  Level 2:  At 7.2m (24 ft)   — 3 guys, 70% radius = 5.0m from base
  Pre-tension each guy to 75–100 N (7.5–10 kgf)

40-FOOT (12m) MAST:
  Level 1:  At 4.0m (13 ft)   — 3 guys, 70% radius = 5.6m from base
  Level 2:  At 8.0m (26 ft)   — 3 guys, 70% radius = 5.6m from base
  Level 3:  At 11.0m (36 ft)  — 3 guys, 70% radius = 5.6m from base
  Pre-tension each guy to 100–150 N (10–15 kgf)

SECTION 5 — RAISING PROCEDURE (2-PERSON MINIMUM FOR >20 FT)
================================================================================

EQUIPMENT REQUIRED:
  - Push stick or gin pole (15-20 ft reach for 30-40 ft mast)
  - Come-along or rope and pulley for raise (>30 ft)
  - Guy wire pre-staged and anchors installed before raising
  - Non-conductive rope tag lines (2) for controlled raise direction

PROCEDURE:
  1. Install all ground anchors before raising.
  2. Pre-attach all guy wires to mast collars (all levels).
  3. Attach antenna hardware at ground level if possible.
  4. Extend all mast sections at ground level; lock each section.
  5. Lay mast along ground pointing toward perpendicular to prevailing wind.
  6. Attach tag lines (non-conductive) 2/3 up mast; two operators on tag lines.
  7. Ginpole or push-stick operator lifts base section; pivoting from base plate.
  8. Tag line operators control swing and prevent over-rotation.
  9. Raise to vertical; seating base in base plate ground spike.
  10. Tension guy Level 3 (top) first; adjust to plumb.
  11. Tension Level 2, then Level 1 working top to bottom.
  12. Final plumb check — mast vertical in both planes ±1°.
  13. Secure all tensioner lock nuts.

PLUMB CHECK METHOD:
  Hang a plumb bob from the top section on a string; measure offset at base.
  Adjust opposing guys until plumb bob centers over base.
  OR: Use a spirit level against multiple sides of base section.
  OR: CYD/MPU-6050 tilt monitor if installed.
