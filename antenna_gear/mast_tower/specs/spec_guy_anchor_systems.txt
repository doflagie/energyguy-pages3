SECTION 1 — ANCHOR FORCE REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

ANCHOR PULLOUT FORCES (minimum required holding capacity):

  From spec_wind_load_calculations.txt:
  Guy wire tension at 70% anchor radius, 70 mph wind:

  Mast Height | Wind Load  | Guy Tension (each, 3 guys) | Required Anchor
  ------------|------------|----------------------------|-----------------
  20 ft (6m)  | 890 N      | 495 N (50 kgf)             | 200 lbf / 890 N
  30 ft (9m)  | 2,000 N    | 1,110 N (113 kgf)          | 500 lbf / 2,230 N
  40 ft (12m) | 3,560 N    | 1,975 N (201 kgf)          | 900 lbf / 4,000 N

  SAFETY FACTOR: Apply 3.0× safety factor to operational load for anchor design.
  Survival (anchor must NOT pull out): 3.0× guy tension values above.

  NOTE: These are per-anchor values for a 3-guy configuration.

SECTION 2 — SCREW ANCHOR SYSTEMS
================================================================================

DESCRIPTION:
  Helical screw anchors (ground screws) thread into soil without driving or
  excavation. Highly portable; reusable. Capacity depends on soil type and
  screw geometry.

TYPE A — LIGHTWEIGHT SCREW ANCHOR (20 ft mast, soft soil):
  Dimensions:  12mm rod, 100mm helical plate diameter, 450mm total length
  Material:    Galvanized steel (zinc coated); 316 SS for coastal/wet soil
  Installation: T-handle driver; 8–12 turns into undisturbed soil
  Capacity:
    Dense clay: 800–1,200 N (80–120 kgf) pullout
    Sandy soil:  400–600 N (40–60 kgf) pullout
    Rocky soil:  Not suitable; use deadman anchor
  Suitable for: 20 ft mast in moderate wind (<40 mph) on firm soil
  Source: Duckbill earth anchor, Power Grip series, or equivalent
  Print: guy_tensioners_anchors.scad: screw_anchor_plate() (T-handle adapter)

TYPE B — MEDIUM SCREW ANCHOR (30 ft mast):
  Dimensions:  16mm rod, 150mm helical plate, 600mm length
  Material:    Galvanized steel
  Capacity:
    Dense clay: 2,000–3,500 N (200–350 kgf)
    Sandy loam: 1,200–1,800 N (120–180 kgf)
  Installation: Steel breaker bar through eye; 2-person twist-in
  Suitable for: 30 ft mast to 50 mph, 40 ft mast to 35 mph

TYPE C — HEAVY SCREW ANCHOR (40 ft mast):
  Dimensions:  22mm rod, 200mm helical plate, 750mm length
  Material:    Hot-dip galvanized or 316 SS
  Capacity:
    Dense clay: 5,000–8,000 N (500–800 kgf)
    Sandy soil: 3,000–4,500 N (300–450 kgf)
  Installation: Drive socket + 1/2" torque wrench or impact driver adapter
  Suitable for: 40 ft mast to 70 mph with 3.0× safety factor on clay

SCREW DEPTH REQUIREMENTS:
  Minimum embedment: 3× helical plate diameter in undisturbed soil
  Avoid: topsoil zone (top 200mm usually disturbed/loose)
  Always drive below frost line for permanent or extended installations

INSTALLATION TORQUE GUIDE (as a capacity check):
  Torque (N·m)  = Kt × Capacity (N)  where Kt ≈ 0.01 for 150mm plate
  For 2,000 N capacity: T = 0.01 × 2,000 = 20 N·m → achievable by hand
  For 5,000 N capacity: T = 50 N·m → need breaker bar or impact driver

FIELD SOIL CLASSIFICATION:
  Soft:       Saturated clay, recent fill, peat → use larger plates, deeper embedment
  Medium:     Firm silt, medium sand, dry topsoil → standard anchor
  Hard:       Dense clay, gravel, compacted fill → any anchor type; easy install
  Rocky:      Bedrock, cobbles → cannot use screw; use stake or deadman on surface

SECTION 3 — STAKE ANCHORS
================================================================================

DESCRIPTION:
  Driven stakes (steel angle, J-stakes, or T-stakes) pounded into soil with a
  mallet. Faster than screw anchors; shallower penetration; lower capacity.

TYPE A — J-STAKE (tent stake style):
  300mm × 10mm galvanized steel rod with bent hook
  Capacity: 300–600 N in firm soil; NOT adequate for >20 ft mast >25 mph
  Suitable for: Very small masts (<15 ft), guy pegs for temporary/calm conditions
  Drive angle: 45° away from mast (load direction) for maximum pullout resistance

TYPE B — T-STAKE:
  400mm × 16mm T-profile galvanized steel
  Capacity: 600–1,200 N in dense soil
  Suitable for: 20 ft mast to 35 mph

TYPE C — DRIVEN ROD ANCHOR:
  600mm × 19mm (3/4") galvanized rebar, bent 90° at top (3" hook)
  Drive with 5 lb engineer's hammer or fence post driver
  Capacity: 1,500–2,500 N in dense clay
  Multiple rods in cluster (3 rods in triangle, 100mm apart): multiply capacity
  Suitable for: 30 ft mast, moderate wind
  Print: guy_tensioners_anchors.scad: stake_anchor_eye() — eye adapter for bent rebar

DRIVING STAKES FOR MAXIMUM CAPACITY:
  Angle: Drive at 60° from horizontal (30° from vertical), angled away from mast
  Depth: Maximum practical depth; surface protrusion 50mm for wire attachment
  No rocks: If stake deflects on rock, relocate; a bent stake has reduced capacity
  Test: After driving, attempt to pull upward by hand; >100 kgf resistance = good

SECTION 4 — SANDBAG ANCHORS
================================================================================

DESCRIPTION:
  When penetrating the ground is not permitted (paved surface, indoor, sensitive
  terrain), sandbag anchors provide a portable alternative. Capacity is limited
  by the weight of sand and friction.

BALLAST CALCULATION:
  Anchor capacity (horizontal) = friction coefficient × total weight
  For sandbag on concrete: μ ≈ 0.6
  Required capacity for 30 ft mast guy at 50 mph: 1,500 N (150 kgf)
  Required sandbag mass: 1,500 / (0.6 × 9.81) = 255 kg

  NOTE: 255 kg of sand is impractical in the field. Sandbag anchors are only
  adequate for:
    - Masts ≤ 20 ft in wind < 25 mph (requires ~45 kg per anchor)
    - In combination with pavement anchors or vehicle parking

SANDBAG ANCHOR DESIGN:
  3× 15 kg military sandbags stacked; total ~45 kg per anchor
  Guy wire threaded under center bag or through a printed D-ring strap
  Print: guy_tensioners_anchors.scad: sandbag_anchor_strap()
  Strap wraps around bag; D-ring accepts guy wire eye or turnbuckle

PAVEMENT ANCHOR (supplement):
  Concrete screw anchor (Tapcon 3/8" × 3.5", 4 per anchor point) into concrete.
  Pullout per Tapcon in 3000 PSI concrete: ~700 N
  4 anchors in a diamond pattern, plate connecting to guy wire: 2,800 N capacity

VEHICLE ANCHOR:
  1" × 3/8" recovery strap looped through vehicle frame or tow hook.
  Vehicle must be parked on level ground with parking brake set.
  Pull direction should NOT align with vehicle departure direction.
  Capacity: Limited by wheel/brake friction → 3,000–10,000 N depending on vehicle
  Do NOT park on soft ground as vehicle may sink under lateral load.

SECTION 5 — DEADMAN ANCHORS
================================================================================

DESCRIPTION:
  A deadman is a buried horizontal plate or tube providing pullout resistance
  through shear failure of a soil column above it. Highest capacity of all
  portable anchor types for given size. Requires excavation.

TYPE A — PLATE DEADMAN:
  600mm × 200mm × 6mm galvanized steel plate
  Bury at 400mm depth; cable through center hole exits to surface
  Backfill and tamp; mark surface location
  Capacity (loose sand, 400mm depth): 3,000–4,500 N
  Capacity (dense clay, 400mm depth): 8,000–15,000 N

TYPE B — TUBE DEADMAN:
  2× 600mm × 32mm galvanized pipe, wired together
  Same installation procedure as plate; better in loose soil
  Capacity: 4,000–7,000 N in medium soil

TYPE C — LOG/TIMBER DEADMAN (field expedient):
  Any hardwood log, 300mm diameter × 600mm length
  Bury at 500mm; cable around center
  Capacity: 5,000–10,000 N in undisturbed soil
  NOT removable/reusable; suitable for long-term field stations

DEADMAN INSTALLATION:
  1. Excavate trench 400–600mm deep × 200mm wide × 600mm long
  2. Lay anchor in trench; route cable up through narrow slit to surface
  3. Backfill in 100mm layers; tamp each layer firmly
  4. Mark location with stake or flagging for retrieval

CABLE ROUTING:
  Use cable through a short section of 25mm PVC conduit at surface to
  prevent soil cutting the cable over time. Conduit angle matches guy angle.

SECTION 6 — ANCHOR SELECTION GUIDE
================================================================================

SURFACE TYPE → RECOMMENDED ANCHOR:

  Field/lawn (soft)        → Type A screw (450mm) + Type B for >30 ft
  Field/lawn (hard)        → Type B or C screw anchor
  Dense clay/compacted     → Type C screw or driven rebar
  Sandy (beach, desert)    → Deadman plate anchor + extra depth
  Rocky/shale              → Surface plate anchor with masonry anchors
  Concrete/asphalt         → Pavement anchor (Tapcon) or vehicle
  Timber (deck/dock)       → Through-bolt with fender washer (M12)
  Indoor (gym floor, etc.) → Sandbag ballast (limit 20 ft, 15 mph)

ANCHOR SPACING FROM BASE:
  Always maintain minimum 0.5× mast height anchor radius.
  Do not place anchor within 1m of existing buried utilities.
  Mark anchor locations with surveyor flags for trip hazard awareness.

ANCHOR INSPECTION:
  Before each use:
    □ Visual inspect anchor body for cracks or deformation
    □ Probe soil at anchor with rod — confirm soil not disturbed
    □ Apply gradual pull (body weight) on guy wire to test anchor
    □ Any movement >10mm under body-weight pull: re-install or supplement
