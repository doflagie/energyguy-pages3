SECTION 1 — SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

DESCRIPTION:
  A telescoping fiberglass mast provides lightweight, non-conductive, corrosion-
  resistant antenna support for portable field operations. Pultruded E-glass
  fiberglass tubing is available in standard OD steps that nest with 3–5mm
  annular clearance for smooth extension and locking.

APPLICATIONS:
  - Vertical antenna support (EFHW, ground-mounted verticals, J-poles)
  - Wire antenna center support or end support
  - Field portable HF/VHF/UHF antenna elevation
  - Satellite antenna temporary masting (QRP, SOTA, POTA)
  - Non-conductive mast for electrically active elements

HEIGHT CONFIGURATIONS:
  20-foot system:  4 sections × 6 ft nominal
  30-foot system:  5 sections × 7 ft nominal
  40-foot system:  6 sections × 8 ft nominal (requires 2-level guying)

WEIGHT (COMPLETE SYSTEM):
  20 ft: ~4 kg (9 lb) without anchors
  30 ft: ~7 kg (15 lb) without anchors
  40 ft: ~11 kg (24 lb) without anchors

SECTION 2 — MATERIAL SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

TUBE MATERIAL:
  Type:     Pultruded E-glass fiberglass (Standard Grade)
  Resin:    Polyester or vinyl ester (vinyl ester preferred for UV resistance)
  Tensile:  ~50,000 psi longitudinal
  Flexural: ~35,000 psi
  Wall:     3.0mm (0.125 in) for all sections

PREFERRED STOCK:
  Supplier: Mcmaster-Carr, DragonPlate, Grainger, or local plastics distributor
  Grade:    Pultruded round tube, natural or olive drab (avoid clear/white for UV)

SECTION SIZING TABLE:
  Section | OD (mm) | ID (mm) | Length  | Fits Inside | Overlap Required
  --------|---------|---------|---------|-------------|------------------
    1 (top)|  32    |  26    |  1.8m   | Section 2   | 300mm min
    2      |  38    |  32    |  1.8m   | Section 3   | 300mm min
    3      |  44    |  38    |  1.8m   | Section 4   | 300mm min
    4      |  50    |  44    |  1.8m   | Section 5   | 300mm min
    5      |  57    |  51    |  1.8m   | Section 6   | 350mm min
    6 (base)|  63   |  57    |  1.8m   | Ground spike| 350mm min

  Clearance between sections: 0.5–1.5mm annular (tight slip fit, not sloppy)

LOCKING METHOD — HOSE CLAMP (primary):
  Each section locked by a stainless steel hose clamp (Jubilee clip) over the
  top of the lower section where the upper section exits.
  Clamp size to suit OD: #24 (44–57mm) or #28 (57–76mm)

LOCKING METHOD — PRINTED COLLAR (secondary):
  See mast_collars.scad: split_collar() module.
  Printed PETG split collar with M4 × 20mm bolts; clamps to lower section OD.
  Acts as positive stop — upper section rests on collar lip; hose clamp secures.

SECTION 4 — ASSEMBLY SEQUENCE
================================================================================

SECTION NESTING (NESTED/TRANSPORT):
  All sections nested from largest (bottom/outside) to smallest (top/inside).
  Nested stack height: ~2.0m × 63mm diameter — fits inside vehicle or long bag.

EXTENSION SEQUENCE:
  1. Stand nested assembly vertically; bottom section on ground/spike.
  2. Extend Section 2 upward from Section 3, lock with hose clamp.
     Leave 300mm overlap. Do not extend to bottom of travel.
  3. Repeat for each successive section, working from bottom to top.
  4. Final Section 1 (top) extended last; antenna hardware attached before
     raising if top-heavy. Attach antenna at ground level.
  5. Install guy ring collars at 1/3 and 2/3 height (or per guying schedule).
  6. Raise mast; tension guys symmetrically.

SECTION EXTENSION TIPS:
  - Wipe mast OD surfaces clean before extension; grit causes binding and wear.
  - Light application of dry PTFE lubricant (Teflon spray) eases extension.
  - Do NOT use petroleum lubricants; degrade fiberglass resin over time.
  - Check each locked section for axial play; retighten hose clamp if >3mm play.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — LOAD RATINGS AND LIMITS
================================================================================

ANTENNA TIP LOAD (concentrated, no guy wires):
  20 ft, Beaufort 4 (15 mph):     1.5 kg tip load maximum
  20 ft, Beaufort 6 (25 mph):     0.5 kg tip load maximum

  NOTE: These ratings are WITHOUT guy wires. See spec_wind_load_calculations.txt
  for guyed mast ratings. Guying dramatically increases allowable loads.

GUYING REQUIRED:
  Height ≥ 20 ft:    1 set of guys at 60% height minimum
  Height ≥ 30 ft:    2 sets of guys at 40% and 80% height
  Height ≥ 40 ft:    3 sets of guys at 30%, 60%, and 90% height

MINIMUM GUY WIRE:
  3mm Dyneema SK75 (breaking strength ~1800 kg) — adequate for all heights.
  3mm galvanized steel (breaking strength ~600 kg) — minimum for 20-30 ft.
  Never use clothesline, paracord, or polypropylene rope as guy wire.

================================================================================
SECTION 6 — MAINTENANCE AND INSPECTION
================================================================================

BEFORE EACH USE:
  □ Inspect each section for cracks, delamination, or crazing (white stress marks)
  □ Check hose clamp screws — replace any with stripped drive slots
  □ Inspect section overlap joints — no grit, chips, or binding
  □ Check guy wire terminal ends (crimps or knots) for slippage or fraying

STORAGE:
  Store nested inside a canvas or nylon tube bag; end caps prevent contamination.
  Avoid prolonged UV exposure when stored (UV degrades polyester resin).
  Vinyl ester resin masts may be stored outdoors indefinitely.

DAMAGE ASSESSMENT:
  Minor surface scratches: acceptable; seal with epoxy if >1mm deep.
  White stress crazing near locking clamps: excessive clamping force or impact;
    reduce clamp torque; remove section from service if crazing extends >50mm.
  Section bend (visible curvature): remove from service; mast is compromised.
  Delamination (layers separating): remove from service immediately.

SECTION 7 — CYD MONITORING (OPTIONAL)
================================================================================

A CYD (Cheap Yellow Display, ESP32 2.8" TFT) can be mounted at the mast base
to provide real-time structural monitoring using the following sensors:

SENSORS:
  Tilt/vibration: MPU-6050 IMU at base of mast
    - Monitor mast lean angle (alert if >5° from vertical)
    - Log wind-induced vibration amplitude
  Strain: load cell under base plate (HX711 + 50kg bar cell)
    - Monitor base vertical load (deadweight + wind drag)
  Anemometer: hall-effect cup anemometer (reed switch pulses)
    - Real-time wind speed → dynamic guy tension recommendation

CYD DISPLAY LAYOUT (320×240):
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  MAST MONITOR    HT: 20 ft         │
  │  Tilt: 1.2°      Wind: 12 mph      │
  │  Base Load: 18 kg                  │
  │  [█████████░░] Tension: NOMINAL    │
  │  VC1: 3.71  VC2: 3.72  VC3: 3.71  │  ← battery if BMS present
  │  [RAISE OK]  [LOWER]  [ALERT: --] │
  └─────────────────────────────────────┘

  Color coding:
    Green:  Tilt < 3°, Wind < 25 mph, tension nominal
    Yellow: Tilt 3–5°, Wind 25–40 mph, adjust required
    Red:    Tilt > 5°, Wind > 40 mph — LOWER MAST IMMEDIATELY

WIRING:
  CYD GPIO: MPU-6050 via I2C (GPIO 22/21); HX711 via GPIO 26/25; anemometer GPIO 34
  Power: 5V USB from field battery pack; or 3-cell LiFePO4 via BMS (TM-BMS-001)
