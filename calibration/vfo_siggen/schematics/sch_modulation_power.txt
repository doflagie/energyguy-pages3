SCHEMATIC: MODULATION, SWEEP, AND POWER SUPPLY
TM-SG-001  Rev A
AM/FM/CW modulation; frequency sweep mode; Li-ion + USB-C power
================================================================================

SECTION A — MODULATION CIRCUITRY
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Three modulation modes:

  AM (Amplitude Modulation):
    Modulates the output amplitude.  The AD9851 DDS has no built-in AM
    control.  AM is implemented by varying the ERA-3SM supply voltage
    (collector modulation) or via the HMC1119 digital attenuator updated
    at audio rate.

  FM (Frequency Modulation):
    Modulates the output frequency.  DDS FM: update FTW at audio rate.
    Si5351 FM: update PLL dividers at audio rate.
    ADF4351 FM: update R0 register INT/FRAC at audio rate.

  CW (Carrier Wave keying):
    On/off switching with shaped envelope (raised cosine) to prevent
    key clicks.  Controlled by external key line or software.

MODULATION INPUT:
  MIC / AUDIO INPUT:
    ESP32 GPIO34 (ADC1_CH6, input-only) ── AF input
    Input: 0–3.3V; bias at 1.65V for AC-coupled audio
    [MIC_IN] ─── [R_in: 10kΩ] ─── [C_coup: 4.7µF] ─── [R_bias: 10kΩ/10kΩ divider to 1.65V] ─── ESP32 GPIO34
    ADC sampling rate: set to 8 kHz for voice, 22 kHz for hi-fi

A.1 AM MODULATION — SUPPLY VOLTAGE METHOD
------------------------------------------
Vary ERA-3SM VCC to modulate amplitude.  Audio envelope controls
a MOSFET in series with the amplifier supply.

  +5V ── [MOSFET Q_am: BS170] ── [R_bias: 270Ω] ── ERA-3SM VCC pin
              │
          [Gate: ESP32 DAC GPIO25]  (8-bit DAC; 0–3.3V)

  For 100% AM:
    ESP32 DAC: output sine wave at audio frequency.
    Modulated voltage at ERA-3SM VCC: varies from ~1.5V (min) to ~5V (max).
    Modulation depth: ~70% (limited by amplifier operating range).

  IMPROVED AM — HMC1119 AUDIO-RATE UPDATE:
    Update HMC1119 attenuation register at audio rate (8 kHz ISR).
    Attenuation = 15 dB + (15 × audio_sample / ADC_max) dB
    Range: 0–31.75 dB, 0.25 dB steps → audio bandwidth to 4 kHz (8 kHz sample/2).
    This provides linear AM with accurate modulation depth.

A.2 FM MODULATION — DDS FREQUENCY UPDATE
------------------------------------------
Update the AD9851 FTW register at audio sampling rate.

  Audio sample → FTW offset = audio_value × K_fm
  Where K_fm = deviation / (ADC_max × f_clk / 2^32)
  For ±3 kHz NBFM deviation, ADC_max = 4096:
    K_fm = 3000 / (4096 × 150e6 / 4294967296) = 3000 / 34.896 = 86 FTW/LSB

  SPI transaction time: ~40 bits at 20 MHz SPI = 2 µs.
  At 8 kHz audio: 1 FTW update per 125 µs → plenty of headroom.

  FM DEVIATION SETTINGS (operator-selectable):
    NBFM: ±2.5 kHz (VHF voice)
    WBFM: ±75 kHz (broadcast FM simulation; at HF frequencies only)
    Tone sweep: ±100 Hz for narrow filter testing
    Carrier: no modulation (pure CW mode)

  Si5351 FM:
    Update set_freq() at audio rate.
    Library call time: ~1 ms (I2C); limits audio bandwidth to ~500 Hz.
    Better: directly write PLL integer/fractional registers via I2C.
    Direct I2C write: ~200 µs; audio bandwidth to ~2.5 kHz.

  ADF4351 FM:
    Write R0 with new FRAC word at audio rate.
    SPI write time: ~200 ns at 20 MHz; audio bandwidth > 10 kHz possible.
    FM deviation: 1 FTW step = f_PD/MOD = 10 MHz/1000 = 10 kHz/step.
    For narrow FM: use larger MOD (up to 4095).
    1 FRAC step = 10 MHz/4095 = 2.44 kHz (minimum deviation step at VHF).

A.3 CW KEYING — SHAPED ENVELOPE
---------------------------------
CW keying switches the output on/off with a raised-cosine envelope to
prevent key clicks.  Rise/fall time: 5 ms (matches ARRL recommendation).

  CW KEY INPUT:
    External key (mechanical or iambic) ── [R_pull: 10kΩ to 3.3V] ── ESP32 GPIO32
    Software: ISR on GPIO32 (key down = LOW; key up = HIGH)

  SHAPED KEYING — ERA-3SM SUPPLY METHOD:
    On key-down: ramp ERA-3SM VCC from 0V to 5V over 5 ms
    On key-up: ramp from 5V to 0V over 5 ms
    Ramp implemented by ESP32 DAC on GPIO25 (25 Hz update rate during ramp = 5ms/200 steps)
    Result: raised-cosine shape approximated by linear ramp on supply.

  SHAPED KEYING — HMC1119 METHOD (preferred):
    On key-down: decrement HMC1119 attenuation from 70 dB to 0 dB over 5 ms
    On key-up: increment from 0 dB to 70 dB over 5 ms
    20 steps × 5 ms = 25 ms total ramp; adjust step count for faster/slower edges.
    ISR at 4 kHz drives the ramp; keyer state machine manages timing.

  CW SIDETONE:
    ESP32 GPIO2 (LEDC channel) → [RC: 1kΩ + 10µF] → audio amplifier → speaker
    Tone frequency: 600–800 Hz (operator adjustable via CYD menu)
    Sidetone level: adjustable via LEDC duty cycle.

AUDIO INPUT JACK:
  3.5 mm stereo jack (J_mic):
    Tip (L): audio input signal
    Ring (R): not connected
    Sleeve: GND

  J_mic ── [C_in: 4.7µF] ── [R1: 10kΩ] ──┬── ESP32 GPIO34 (AF input ADC)
                                           │
                                    [R2: 10kΩ] ── 1.65V bias (3.3V/2 divider)

SECTION B — FREQUENCY SWEEP MODE
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Sweep mode steps the output frequency from a start frequency to a stop
frequency at a user-defined step size and dwell time.  Used for:
  - Filter response measurement (plot insertion loss vs. frequency)
  - Antenna SWR sweep (with external SWR bridge)
  - Crystal filter passband characterization
  - Low-pass filter prototype verification

SWEEP PARAMETERS (operator-configured on CYD):
  Start frequency: any tunable frequency
  Stop frequency:  any tunable frequency (> start)
  Step size:       1 Hz to 1 MHz (logarithmic or linear)
  Dwell time:      1 ms to 1 s per step
  Sweep mode:      linear / log / ping-pong (forward-reverse-forward)
  Trigger:         manual (button) / continuous / external (GPIO)
  Output:          serial (for PC plotting) / CYD display (bar graph)

SWEEP EXECUTION (firmware logic):
  1. Start at f_start.  Set synthesizer to f_start.  Wait dwell_time.
  2. Read AD8307 output level; record (freq, level_dBm) pair.
  3. Step to f_start + step_size.
  4. Repeat until f_stop reached.
  5. Output data as CSV over USB serial:  freq_Hz,level_dBm

CYD DISPLAY DURING SWEEP:
  Real-time bar graph: X = frequency (linear or log), Y = dBm.
  Cursor can be placed to read specific frequency/level values.
  Markers: M1 and M2 for −3 dB bandwidth measurement.
  Stores last sweep in RAM for comparison with current sweep.

EXTERNAL TRIGGER:
  ESP32 GPIO32 ── [10kΩ pull-up] ── trigger input (step on rising edge)
  Allows external device (NanoVNA, logic analyzer) to sync the sweep.

SWEEP CALCULATION (firmware):
  For linear sweep:
    f_current = f_start + step_index × step_size
    num_steps = (f_stop − f_start) / step_size
  For log sweep:
    f_current = f_start × (f_stop/f_start)^(step_index / num_steps)
    step_size recalculated each step.

SWEEP PERFORMANCE:
  DDS (AD9851): FTW update via SPI, ~2 µs per step.  Min dwell: 5 ms.
  Si5351: I2C PLL update, ~200 µs per step.  Min dwell: 10 ms.
  ADF4351: SPI + lock time, ~500 µs per step.  Min dwell: 2 ms.
  AD8307 settling: 10 µs (log amp bandwidth 500 MHz).

  At 1 ms dwell (DDS): 1000 steps per second → 1 kHz to 40 MHz in 39 seconds.
  At 5 ms dwell: comfortable for 200-step sweep across a ham band.

SECTION C — POWER SUPPLY
================================================================================

POWER REQUIREMENTS:
  Component           Voltage  Current (typical)  Peak
  ---------           -------  -----------------  ----
  ESP32 + CYD         3.3V     240 mA             500 mA (WiFi)
  Si5351              3.3V     30 mA              50 mA
  AD9851              3.3V     90 mA              120 mA
  ADF4351             3.3V     150 mA             200 mA
  ERA-3SM amplifier   5.0V     60 mA              100 mA
  HMC1119             3.3V     25 mA              40 mA
  AD8307              5.0V     8 mA               10 mA
  Relays (K1–K7, 5V)  5.0V     35 mA × 7 = 245 mA (all on simultaneously)
  LM7805 bias etc.    var      20 mA              30 mA
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  3.3V rail total:    ~600 mA (all active, no WiFi)
  5.0V rail total:    ~350 mA (amps + relays)
  Overall:            ~2.3 W typical; 4.5 W worst case

POWER ARCHITECTURE:
  USB-C (5V in) ──┬── [MCP73831: LiPo charger] ── 3.7V LiPo 2000 mAh
                  │
  LiPo 3.7V ──────┼── [TPS61023: 5V boost, 2A] ── 5V rail
                  │
                  └── [AMS1117-3.3: 3.3V LDO] ── 3.3V rail

  5V BOOST — TPS61023 (Texas Instruments):
    Input: 2.3–5.5V (LiPo or USB-C direct)
    Output: 5.0V ± 1.5%, 2A max
    Efficiency: 92% typical at 500 mA
    Enable: GPIO control from ESP32 for power management
    Schematic:
      V_in ── TPS61023 IN ── [L1: 2.2µH] ── V_out (5V)
      [C_in: 10µF] to GND
      [C_out: 22µF + 100nF] to GND
      Feedback resistors: R1=100kΩ, R2=22.6kΩ → V_out=5.0V

  3.3V LDO — AMS1117-3.3 (800 mA max):
    Input: 5V rail; Output: 3.3V
    [C_in: 10µF] [C_out: 10µF + 100nF]
    Heat dissipation: (5.0 − 3.3) × 0.6 A = 1.02 W → add heatsink or
    use TPS62203 switching regulator (smaller, more efficient).

LIFEPO4 BATTERY OPTION (safer than LiPo):
  LiFePO4 18650 cell: 3.2V nominal, 3.65V full charge, 2.5V cutoff.
  Use two cells in series for 6.4V; regulate down to 5V.
  Better for field use; no thermal runaway risk; 5-year shelf life.
  Charger: CN3795 (6V input MPPT charger, also works with wall adapter).

USB-C POWER DELIVERY:
  USB-C connector (5.1kΩ CC resistors for 5V/900mA identification).
  Power path: USB-C VBUS → [D_oring: PRTR5V0U2X or MOSFET OR-gate] → LiPo charger + boost.
  When USB connected: charges battery AND powers circuit.
  When USB disconnected: battery powers via boost.

  USB-C SCHEMATIC:
    USB-C VBUS ── [D_in: B5819W Schottky] ── power bus → [MCP73831 VCC]
    USB-C CC1 ── [R_cc1: 5.1kΩ] ── GND
    USB-C CC2 ── [R_cc2: 5.1kΩ] ── GND
    (5.1kΩ on CC identifies as 5V sink device to host/charger)

POWER SWITCH AND PROTECTION:
  POWER SWITCH: SPST toggle on supply rail after boost output.
  REVERSE POLARITY: P-MOSFET (Si2333DS) with gate tied to GND → source to BATT+.
  OVER-VOLTAGE: TVS diode (SMBJ5.0A) across 5V rail.
  LOW BATTERY: AEM10941 energy harvesting / LT1540 cell voltage monitor.
    ESP32 reads voltage divider [47kΩ/10kΩ] on battery → GPIO35 ADC.
    V_batt_mV = ADC_mV × (47+10)/10
    Alert when V_batt < 3.0V (LiPo); shut down at 2.8V.

POWER SUPPLY SCHEMATIC (complete):
  USB-C ─────────────────────────────────────── VBUS (5V)
              │
        [MCP73831T]  ← lithium charger IC (SOT-23-5)
              │
        [LiPo/LiFePO4 2000mAh cell]
              │
        [TPS61023 boost]  → 5V rail
              │
        [AMS1117-3.3 LDO] → 3.3V rail
              │
        [47kΩ/10kΩ divider] → ESP32 ADC (battery monitor)
