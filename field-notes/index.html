Field Notes

Operational field procedures for portable HF/VHF operations, site characterization, and propagation observation. KO6NNH, Merced CA.

California RF Site Assessment

TM-FIELD-CA-001. Characterize any California operating site before antenna deployment. Covers geological interference, ground conductivity (σ), noise floor, and path assessment using NanoVNA and TinySA. Province quick-reference from Central Valley to Death Valley.

Sierra Nevada RF Site Assessment

TM-FIELD-SAP-001. Sierra-specific site assessment procedure. Granite batholith, contact zones, seasonal σ variation, and antenna selection for very poor ground (<2 mS/m).
