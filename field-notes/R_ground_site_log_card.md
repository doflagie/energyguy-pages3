**SEASONAL COMPLETION CHECKLIST** *(one entry per site per season)*

- □ Winter/early spring  (Jan–Mar)  — peak conductivity
- □ Late spring          (Apr–May)  — declining
- □ Late summer          (Aug–Sep)  — minimum conductivity
- □ After first rain     (Oct–Nov)  — recovering

*A complete annual cycle for a site = all four boxes checked.*
*Three sites × four seasons = 12 cards for a full local σ map.*
