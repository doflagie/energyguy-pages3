CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers the design, construction, and calibration of a standalone capacitance meter. The instrument measures capacitance from 1 pF to 10,000 µF using charge/discharge timing or RC oscillator methods. Key applications include capacitor sorting, value verification, and electrolytic capacitor ESR estimation.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-015 (LCR Meter), TM-TGEAR-017 (Multimeter DMM), TM-CAL-011 (RC Time Constant).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. CHARGE/DISCHARGE TIMING METHOD

A precision current source charges the unknown capacitor CX until the voltage reaches a threshold VT. The charging time t is proportional to CX:

Capacitance from current and timeCX = I × t / VT

MCU timer captures t with µs resolution. For large capacitors, a smaller current source (nA range) extends the range to 10,000 µF.

2-2. RC OSCILLATOR METHOD

A 555 or comparator oscillator with known R and unknown CX generates a frequency proportional to 1/CX. A frequency counter measures f, then computes CX = 1/(1.44 × R × f) for the 555 astable.

555 astable frequencyf = 1.44 / ((RA + 2RB) × C)

CHAPTER 3 — CONSTRUCTION

3-1. PRECISION CURRENT SOURCE

Use a JFET current source (BF245 or 2N5457) or a precision op-amp with resistor feedback. Target current: 1 mA for small capacitors (pF–nF), 10 µA for µF range, 100 nA for 10 mF range.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Build and verify current source: connect DMM in series and verify I within 1% of design value at 2.5 V across dummy capacitor.
  2. Connect MCU timer input to comparator output. Set comparator threshold to 2.5 V (half VCC).
  3. Calibrate with known NPO/C0G capacitors (100 pF, 1 nF, 10 nF) before enabling auto-ranging.
  4. Install zero-adjustment: short input and press ZERO to subtract parasitic wiring capacitance (typically 2–10 pF).

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Zero the instrument: short test leads, press ZERO. Parasitic capacitance should be subtracted.
  2. Connect C0G 100 pF ±1% reference capacitor. Reading must be 100 ± 5 pF.
  3. Connect 1 nF ±1% reference. Reading must be 1.00 ± 0.05 nF.
  4. Connect 100 nF ±1% film reference. Reading must be 100 ± 5 nF.
  5. Connect 1 µF ±1% film reference. Reading must be 1.00 ± 0.05 µF.
  6. If out of tolerance, adjust gain constant in firmware (k = I / VT).

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Accuracy depends on calibration of the current source (I) and the threshold voltage (VT). Adjust VT via resistor divider ratio. Adjust I via feedback resistor in op-amp current mirror. Temperature coefficient of film resistors (<50 ppm/°C) limits drift to <0.5% over 25 °C change.
