CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of a noise figure measurement system using the Y-factor method. Instruments covered: noise source (calibrated ENR), spectrum analyzer or power meter as receiver. Applicable to LNA evaluation, receiver sensitivity estimation, and preamplifier characterization from 1 MHz to 1 GHz.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-028 (TinySA Spectrum Analyzer), TM-TGEAR-023 (RF Power Meter), TM-CAL-014 (Dummy Load).

CAUTIONNoise sources may produce DC bias on the output (typically +28 V DC on/off) for ENR control. Do not connect the noise source output directly to inputs rated for DC-blocked operation only. Use a DC block (bias-T blocking cap) in series if required.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. Y-FACTOR METHOD

A calibrated noise source presents two noise temperatures: Thot (noise ON) and Tcold (noise OFF, approximately T0 = 290 K). The Y-factor is:

Y factorY = Phot / Pcold [power ratio, not dB]
Noise figure from Y-factorNF = 10 log10((ENR / (Y−1)) − (T0 × (1−Y)) / (Y−1) / T0)

For ENR = 15 dB (factor 31.6) and Y = 5 dB (factor 3.16): NF ≈ 1.0 dB.

2-2. EXCESS NOISE RATIO

ENR (noise source)ENR = 10 log10((Thot − Tcold) / T0) [T0 = 290 K]

CHAPTER 3 — CONSTRUCTION

3-1. HOMEBREW NOISE SOURCE

A reverse-biased avalanche diode (BFR90, MMBR920, or similar) with 20–30 V reverse bias produces broadband noise with ENR ≈ 15–20 dB at HF/VHF.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Build noise diode biasing circuit on RF PCB. Use ground plane, keep all RF leads <5 mm.
  2. Apply 28 V through 1 kΩ to reverse-biased diode junction. Measure current: should be 2–5 mA in avalanche breakdown.
  3. Verify broadband noise on spectrum analyzer from 10–500 MHz: noise floor should rise >10 dB above analyzer noise floor.
  4. Measure ENR against known commercial noise source or calibrated power standard if available. Else assign ENR = 15 dB nominal and use with 1–2 dB uncertainty.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Set TinySA to zero-span, BW 100 kHz, at desired frequency (e.g., 144 MHz).
  2. Connect noise source output (via 20 dB pad) to TinySA input. Switch noise source OFF. Record Pcold (dBm).
  3. Switch noise source ON. Record Phot (dBm).
  4. Y = 10(Phot−Pcold)/10. Compute NF per formula in Section 2-1.
  5. Insert DUT (LNA) between noise source and TinySA. Repeat measurement. Compute DUT noise figure.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

ENR calibration accuracy is the primary uncertainty. Calibrate noise source ENR against a precision spectrum analyzer at multiple frequencies (10, 50, 100, 200, 500 MHz). Record ENR vs. frequency table. Use frequency-specific ENR values for each measurement frequency.

APPENDIX A — CALCULATIONS AND FORMULAS

Y-factor (linear)Y = Phot / Pcold = 10(Phot(dBm) − Pcold(dBm))/10
Noise temperatureTe = T0 × (10ENR/10 − Y) / (Y − 1) [K]
Noise figureNF = 10 log10(1 + Te/T0) [dB]
Friis formula (cascaded stages)NFtotal ≈ NF1 + (NF2−1)/G1 [linear quantities]
