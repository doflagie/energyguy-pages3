CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of a precision constant-current source intended for Kelvin (4-wire) resistance measurement, electrochemical sensing, and current loop calibration. Output currents covered: 1 mA, 10 mA, 100 mA.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-017 (Multimeter DMM), TM-CAL-010 (Kelvin 4-Wire Resistance), TM-CAL-008 (Precision Resistor Network), TM-CAL-006 (Precision Voltage Reference).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. VOLTAGE-TO-CURRENT CONVERSION

A precision op-amp forces the voltage across a sense resistor RS equal to a reference voltage VREF. The resulting current I = VREF/RS flows through the load independent of load voltage (within compliance range).

Output currentIOUT = VREF / RS

2-2. COMPLIANCE VOLTAGE

The current source maintains regulation only while VLOAD < VSUPPLY − VDROPOUT. For a 12 V supply and 1.5 V dropout, maximum load resistance at 10 mA is 1050 Ω.

Maximum load resistanceRLOAD(max) = (VSUPPLY − VDROPOUT − VREF) / IOUT

CHAPTER 3 — CONSTRUCTION

3-1. HOWLAND CURRENT PUMP

The improved Howland current pump uses four matched 10 kΩ resistors around a precision op-amp (OPA2134) to produce a floating current source with high output impedance (>10 MΩ). Resistor matching to 0.1% is critical.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Match four 10 kΩ resistors: measure each with calibrated 4-wire DMM. Select pairs within 1 Ω of each other.
  2. Solder op-amp on DIP socket to allow replacement without rework.
  3. Install VREF chip. Measure VREF with calibrated DMM; record actual value for IOUT calculation.
  4. Connect 100 Ω load. Measure load current with series precision shunt resistor + DMM. Adjust sense resistor if needed.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Connect 1.000 Ω ±0.01% 4-terminal resistor as load (TM-CAL-010 standard).
  2. Measure voltage across 1 Ω resistor with precision DMM. V = I × 1 Ω, so V numerically equals I in mA.
  3. Adjust VREF trimmer or RS until measured current = 10.000 mA ± 0.01 mA.
  4. Repeat across temperature range (0–50 °C) if operating in field conditions.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Fine-tune output current by trimming RS or using a precision multi-turn potentiometer in series with the fixed RS. Temperature drift of 10 Ω wirewound sense resistors is <10 ppm/°C, limiting IOUT drift to <10 ppm/°C.
