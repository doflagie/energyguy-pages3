CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers the calibration procedures for the FNIRSI OSCB35 100 MHz digital oscilloscope and equivalent portable digital storage oscilloscopes (DSO). Calibrations covered: vertical scale (voltage accuracy), timebase (time/div), probe compensation, and trigger threshold.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-CAL-006 (Precision Voltage Reference), TM-CAL-002 (GPS 1PPS), TM-TGEAR-017 (Fluke 15B+ DMM).

WARNINGIf the oscilloscope is AC-mains powered, its BNC input grounds are connected to the power-supply chassis ground. Connecting the BNC ground clip to any non-earth-referenced circuit creates a shock hazard and can damage the circuit under test. Use a battery-powered oscilloscope for floating measurements, or use an isolation transformer on the oscilloscope power supply.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. ANALOG FRONT END

Input signal passes through an attenuator (for high-sensitivity settings), an AC/DC coupling switch, and a bandwidth-limiting filter. A buffer amplifier (typically 1 GHz GBP, e.g., AD8065) drives the ADC.

2-2. ADC AND MEMORY

The FNIRSI OSCB35 uses a 12-bit ADC at 250 MSPS. Vertical resolution is 12 bits = 1/4096 of full scale. Effective Number of Bits (ENOB) in practice is 9–10 bits due to ADC noise and distortion.

2-3. TIMEBASE

The timebase is generated from a crystal oscillator (typically 24 or 48 MHz TCXO in portable DSOs). The displayed time/div = (sample points × ADC period) / divisions. Accuracy depends on crystal stability (±50 ppm for a standard crystal).

2-4. PROBE COMPENSATION

A 10:1 probe uses a voltage divider (9 MΩ tip resistor in series with 1 MΩ scope input). A trim capacitor Ccomp in the probe tip compensates for cable and scope input capacitance to maintain flat frequency response.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Probe compensation: connect probe to PROBE COMP output on oscilloscope (usually 1 kHz, 2 V square wave). Adjust probe Ccomp trimmer until square wave top is flat (no overshoot or rounding). Use non-metallic adjustment tool.
  2. Verify self-calibration: run built-in CAL routine (if present in FNIRSI firmware). This corrects internal ADC gain and offset.
  3. Power oscilloscope from battery (if possible) to avoid AC mains ground interference.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

5-1. VERTICAL SCALE (VOLTAGE) CALIBRATION

  1. Apply 2.500 V DC from LM4040 reference to CH1 via BNC cable. Set scope to DC coupling, 1 V/div.
  2. Trigger on CH1. Measure displayed voltage. Correction factor = 2.500 / Vdisplayed.
  3. Repeat at 500 mV/div and 2 V/div. Log correction per range. FNIRSI spec: ±3% typical.

5-2. TIMEBASE CALIBRATION

  1. Apply GPS 1 PPS signal (TM-CAL-002) to CH1. 1 PPS = 1.000000000 s period, 100 ms pulse high.
  2. Set timebase to 200 ms/div. Measure displayed pulse period. Error = (Tdisplayed − 1.000) / 1.000 × 106 ppm.
  3. Alternatively, apply 60 Hz AC mains (via isolating capacitor 1 nF) and verify period = 16.67 ms ±0.01%.
  4. At fast timebase (10 μs/div), apply 10 kHz signal from GPS-calibrated function generator. Verify period = 100 μs ±0.1%.

5-3. BANDWIDTH VERIFICATION

  1. Apply −3 dB frequency from signal generator. For FNIRSI OSCB35, spec is 100 MHz (−3 dB). Apply 100 MHz sine and verify that 10 MHz reading amplitude × 0.707 ≈ 100 MHz reading.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

If vertical accuracy is off >5%, the input attenuator trim capacitors may need adjustment (internal, service access). Timebase accuracy is determined by crystal; replace with TCXO module for <2 ppm accuracy. Probe compensation trim is the only user-accessible mechanical adjustment.
