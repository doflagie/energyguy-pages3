CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of a manual AC impedance bridge (modified Maxwell or Hay bridge) for measuring inductance, capacitance, and resistance at audio frequencies (100 Hz to 100 kHz). The null-balance method provides accuracies of 0.1% with calibrated standards.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-015 (LCR Meter), TM-TGEAR-002 (Audio Oscillator), TM-CAL-008 (Precision Resistor), TM-CAL-010 (Kelvin 4-Wire).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. WHEATSTONE/MAXWELL BRIDGE BALANCE

Four impedance arms (Z1, Z2, Z3, ZX) form a bridge. AC null is detected when the ratio Z1/Z2 = Z3/ZX. At null, no current flows through the detector, and ZX is computed from the known arms.

Maxwell bridge (inductance)LX = R2 × R3 × C1 and RX = R2 × R3 / R1

2-2. HAY BRIDGE

The Hay bridge is preferred for high-Q inductors:

Hay bridge (high-Q inductor)LX = R2 × R3 × C1 / (1 + (1/Q)2)

2-3. NULL DETECTOR

An audio frequency amplifier + earphone or a galvanometer-type null detector senses bridge balance. The detector amplifier must be narrow-band to reject harmonics and improve null sensitivity.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Wire bridge arms on phenolic strip board. Use shielded leads for ZX arm to minimize stray capacitance pickup.
  2. Install 1 kHz source and null detector connections on front panel.
  3. Verify bridge balance with ZX = Z3 (known standard): null should be <1% of drive voltage.
  4. Test with 100 µH inductor on Maxwell bridge: balance for minimum headphone null.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Verify R2 and R3 ratio arms with 4-wire DMM. Record actual values R2/R3 ratio.
  2. Calibrate standard capacitor C1 (decade box) against LCR meter values.
  3. Connect known 100 µH ±0.1% inductor as ZX. Balance bridge. Compute LX = R2R3C1. Compare to known value.
  4. Error should be <0.5%. If not, re-measure R2, R3, C1 individually.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Source frequency affects bridge balance in the Hay configuration. Use 1 kHz for general work; for high-Q iron-core inductors, use lower frequency (100–400 Hz) to avoid skin effect errors. For capacitor measurement, switch to Schering bridge topology.
