CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of an SWR meter (standing wave ratio bridge) for HF and VHF amateur radio operation. The SWR meter measures forward and reflected power, computes SWR, and provides a visual indication of antenna system match quality.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-023 (RF Power Meter), TM-TGEAR-008 (Dummy Load), TM-CAL-014 (Dummy Load), TM-CAL-015 (Attenuator Pads).

CAUTIONDo not leave high SWR (>3:1) loads on a transmitter for extended periods. At SWR 3:1, reflected power is 25% of forward power, returning to the final amplifier and increasing its dissipation. Maximum operating SWR for most solid-state final amplifiers: <2:1.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. DIRECTIONAL BRIDGE (BRUENE COUPLER)

The Bruene coupler uses a toroidal transformer to sample current and a voltage divider to sample voltage on the transmission line. By adding and subtracting these samples, separate forward (VF) and reflected (VR) voltages are obtained.

SWR from VF and VRSWR = (VF + VR) / (VF − VR)
Reflection coefficientΓ = VR / VF

2-2. METER CIRCUIT

Schottky diodes (1N5711 or BAT85) rectify the sampled forward and reflected voltages. DC-coupled meter movements display the power. An analog ratio circuit or MCU computes and displays SWR.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Wind 10 turns of #28 enameled wire through FT-50-43 bead. Pass through-line (coax center conductor) through core as 1-turn primary.
  2. Terminate secondary with 50 Ω load on far end. Connect forward detector to one end and reflected detector to other end via bridge balance resistors.
  3. Install meter movements in series with detector output. Set FSD = 100 W forward power via calibration resistors.
  4. Verify balance: apply signal and short antenna port. Reflected power meter should read full scale; forward should drop appropriately.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Connect 50 Ω precision dummy load to antenna port. Apply 10 W from transmitter.
  2. CAL switch to FWD. Adjust CAL pot for full-scale deflection on forward meter.
  3. Switch to SWR. Meter should read 1.0 (minimum deflection) with 50 Ω load.
  4. Connect 150 Ω load (3:1 SWR). Meter must read 3.0 ±0.2.
  5. Verify directivity: terminate antenna port with 50 Ω dummy load. Apply signal at transmitter port. REF meter reading must be <5% of FWD reading (directivity >26 dB).

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Bridge balance is adjusted by the 1 kΩ trim resistors in the coupling network. Optimal balance (maximum directivity) occurs when the voltage sample Vvolt and current sample Vcurr amplitudes are equal at the detectors. Adjust trimmers with a signal generator and terminated load for minimum reflected power reading.
