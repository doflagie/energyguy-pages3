CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers the design, construction, and calibration of a swept-frequency antenna analyzer. The antenna analyzer measures complex impedance (R + jX), SWR, and reflection coefficient across the HF/VHF spectrum (typically 1–60 MHz). It provides a simplified, portable alternative to a full vector network analyzer.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-018 (NanoVNA), TM-CAL-015 (Attenuator Pads), TM-CAL-014 (Dummy Load), TM-TGEAR-027 (SWR Meter).

1-3. SAFETY SUMMARY

CAUTIONMaximum input RF power is typically 0 dBm (1 mW). Never connect an antenna analyzer to a transmitter output. Transmitter power will destroy the bridge and DDS circuits.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. DIRECTIONAL BRIDGE PRINCIPLE

The antenna analyzer drives a Wheatstone-style RF bridge with an internal DDS (Direct Digital Synthesis) oscillator. The bridge compares the device under test (DUT) impedance against a precision 50 Ω reference. Quadrature sampling of the forward and reflected voltages yields the complex reflection coefficient Γ.

Reflection coefficientΓ = (ZL − Z0) / (ZL + Z0)
SWR from reflection coefficientSWR = (1 + |Γ|) / (1 − |Γ|)

2-2. DDS FREQUENCY SYNTHESIS

A DDS chip (e.g., AD9850 or Si5351) generates a swept sine wave from 1 to 60 MHz. The MCU steps frequency in user-defined increments. Each step acquires I/Q samples from the bridge, computing |Γ| and phase to reconstruct R and X.

2-3. IMPEDANCE EXTRACTION

Complex impedance from ΓZ = Z0 × (1 + Γ) / (1 − Γ)

The resistive component R and reactive component X are displayed directly. Positive X indicates inductive reactance; negative X indicates capacitive reactance.

3-2. RF BRIDGE CONSTRUCTION

Wind a binocular ferrite core (BN-43-202) with 10 turns of 26 AWG enameled wire for the toroidal transformer. Use 51 Ω (1%, 0.1W) metal-film resistors for bridge arms. Keep all bridge wiring under 10 mm to minimize parasitics above 30 MHz.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Wind RF bridge transformer on BN-43-202 core: 10 bifilar turns, twist ratio 1:1.
  2. Solder 51 Ω bridge resistors with leads under 5 mm. Verify balance with DMM before populating MCU.
  3. Program Si5351 with calibration offset per manufacturer data sheet. Default correction 0 ppm.
  4. Connect AD8302 output pins to MCU ADC inputs. Set MCU ADC reference to 3.3 V stable reference.
  5. Power on. Verify DDS output with oscilloscope or frequency counter at SMA port before connecting antenna.
  6. Perform open/short/load calibration (Section 5-1) before first measurements.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

5-1. ONE-PORT CALIBRATION (OSL)

NOTEOSL calibration removes systematic errors in the bridge and cable. Perform OSL at the measurement reference plane (antenna connector).
  1. Connect precision 50 Ω load (TM-CAL-014). Record |Γ|LOAD at 1, 7, 14, 21, 28 MHz. Ideal: |Γ| < 0.01.
  2. Connect precision open circuit (SMA cap or open connector). Record |Γ|OPEN and phase. Ideal: |Γ| = 1.0, phase = 0°.
  3. Connect precision short circuit (SMA shorting cap). Record |Γ|SHORT and phase. Ideal: |Γ| = 1.0, phase = −180°.
  4. Enter calibration data into firmware. MCU computes error correction coefficients ED, ES, ER.
  5. Verify calibration with 100 Ω resistor: analyzer should read R = 100, X = 0, SWR = 2.0:1.

5-2. FREQUENCY AXIS CALIBRATION

Tune to WWV 10.000 MHz. Compare displayed frequency against known. Adjust Si5351 crystal frequency correction (ppm trim in firmware) until display reads 10.000 MHz ± 100 Hz. Alternatively use GPS 1PPS reference per TM-CAL-002.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

6-1. BRIDGE BALANCE

With 50 Ω load connected, adjust bridge balance trimmer (if present) for minimum |Γ|. Typical adjustment range ±5 Ω on the reference arm. An unbalanced bridge produces a frequency-dependent offset in the resistance readout.

6-2. FREQUENCY CORRECTION

Si5351 crystal correction is stored as a signed integer (ppm × 100). Adjust in firmware to match GPS-locked reference. Typical correction range: ±100 ppm depending on crystal quality.

CHAPTER 7 — VERIFICATION

  1. Connect 50 Ω load: SWR must read 1.00 ± 0.05, R = 50 ± 2 Ω, X = 0 ± 2 Ω.
  2. Connect 100 Ω load: SWR must read 2.00 ± 0.1, R = 100 ± 5 Ω.
  3. Connect open: SWR must read >20:1, |Γ| > 0.98.
  4. Connect known inductance (e.g., 10 μH at 7 MHz = +j 440 Ω): verify X reading ± 10%.
