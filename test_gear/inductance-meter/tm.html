CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of a standalone inductance meter for measuring coils and transformers from 100 nH to 100 H. Methods covered: resonance method (L from known C and measured f), and integration method (L from V and dI/dt).

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-015 (LCR Meter), TM-TGEAR-006 (Decade Inductance Box), TM-TGEAR-013 (Impedance Bridge).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. RESONANCE METHOD

A reference capacitor Cref of known value is placed in parallel with the unknown inductor. The oscillator (555 or Colpitts) finds resonant frequency fr. Then:

Inductance from resonanceLX = 1 / ((2πfr)2 × Cref)

2-2. TIME-DOMAIN INTEGRATION

Apply a known voltage step V across L. Measure time Δt for current to rise to threshold IT:

Inductance from rise timeL = V × Δt / IT

2-3. SELF-RESONANCE EFFECT

All inductors have a self-resonant frequency fSRF above which they appear capacitive. The resonance method overestimates L if fr > fSRF/3.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Install 4 reference capacitors on range switch. Measure each with LCR meter and record actual values.
  2. Wire oscillator input across DUT terminals. Verify oscillator starts on each range with 100 μH through 10 mH test inductors.
  3. Program MCU with Cref actual measured values for each range.
  4. Zero meter: short test terminals, press ZERO to subtract wiring/stray inductance (typically 0.1–2 μH).

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Verify reference capacitors with LCR meter. Update firmware constants if measured values differ from nominal by >0.1%.
  2. Measure decade inductance box (TM-TGEAR-006) at positions 10 µH, 100 μH, 1 mH, 10 mH. Results must agree within ±2%.
  3. Measure known 100 μH ±1% standard inductor. Record result.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

If calibration error exceeds 2%, remeasure reference capacitors at 10 kHz with LCR meter and update constants. Temperature coefficient of C0G capacitors is <30 ppm/°C, resulting in <0.1% error over 25 °C range.
