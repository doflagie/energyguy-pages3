CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers construction and calibration of an RF probe for measuring RF voltage at circuit nodes with an oscilloscope or DMM. Two designs are covered: a simple peak detector probe (diode + capacitor, DC output to DMM) and an active 50 Ω buffer probe for oscilloscope use.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-017 (DMM), TM-TGEAR-020 (Oscilloscope), TM-CAL-006 (Voltage Reference).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. PEAK DETECTOR PROBE

A high-frequency diode (1N60 germanium or BAT85 Schottky) rectifies the RF signal. A hold capacitor (100 nF) maintains the peak voltage at the DMM input. The output DC voltage VDC ≈ Vpeak,RF − Vdiode,forward. Germanium diode forward drop ≈ 0.2 V; Schottky ≈ 0.2–0.3 V.

RMS from peak (sine wave)VRMS = (VDC + Vf) / √2

2-2. ACTIVE BUFFER PROBE

A JFET source follower (BF245C) provides high input impedance (>10 MΩ) and low output impedance (<100 Ω) for driving coaxial cable to an oscilloscope. Bandwidth is limited by JFET capacitance (Cgs ≈ 5 pF).

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Solder BAT85 diode in a small copper-clad PCB (10 × 10 mm), anode to probe tip, cathode to hold capacitor.
  2. Install 100 nF hold cap between cathode and ground. Install 10 MΩ bleeder resistor in parallel with cap.
  3. Connect output (cathode side) to DMM input lead. Connect ground to DMM COM lead.
  4. House in heat-shrink tubing with only probe tip exposed.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Apply known RF signal (e.g., 7 MHz, 1.0 VRMS = 2.83 Vpp) from calibrated signal generator.
  2. Measure probe output with DMM DC range. Expected: VDC = Vpeak − Vf = 1.414 − 0.25 = 1.164 V.
  3. Compute calibration factor K = VRMS_true / VDC. Apply this factor to all subsequent readings.
  4. Verify frequency response: apply same VRMS at 1 MHz and 30 MHz. Output should not vary >10%.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

For improved accuracy, replace simple peak detector with a precision rectifier op-amp circuit (AD8307 logarithmic detector). The AD8307 provides calibrated dBm output from −75 to +17 dBm with ±1 dB accuracy at 100 MHz.
