CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers the construction and calibration of a decade inductance box providing switchable inductance from 1 µH to 111.1 mH in decade steps. Used as a known inductance standard for filter design, inductor Q measurement, and LCR meter verification.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-014 (Inductance Meter), TM-TGEAR-015 (LCR Meter), TM-TGEAR-021 (Q Meter).

NOTEInductors in a decade box have mutual inductance coupling between coils on adjacent switch positions. Keep coils physically separated and orient axes perpendicular to minimize coupling errors (<0.1%).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. SERIES INDUCTANCE SWITCHING

Unlike capacitors (which add in parallel), the decade box connects inductors in series. Each switched-in inductor adds its value to the total.

Series inductance (no coupling)Ltotal = L1 + L2 + ... + Ln

2-2. Q AND WINDING RESISTANCE

Each inductor has a winding resistance RDC. The Q factor at frequency f is:

Q factorQ = 2πfL / RDC

For air-core coils on phenolic forms, Q typically ranges from 50 to 200 at 100 kHz.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Wind each inductor using Neumann formula (see Appendix A). Measure inductance with LCR meter and adjust turns for ±2% of target.
  2. Mount coils in metal enclosure with coil axes perpendicular to each other. Minimum spacing 20 mm.
  3. Use rotary switches rated for inductance service (silver contacts, low contact resistance <5 mΩ).
  4. Connect ground lug to enclosure. Shield each decade section with thin copper sheet if mutual coupling is observed.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Set decade box to position 0 (no inductors connected). Zero-subtract LCR meter.
  2. Set each position 1–9 of each decade. Measure L and RDC at 10 kHz with LCR meter.
  3. Record measured vs. design values. Accept ±2%.
  4. Measure self-resonant frequency of highest value (10 mH) with VNA: confirm fSRF > 50 kHz.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Adjust inductance by adding or removing turns. One turn on a T68-2 core at 100 µH adds approximately 0.4 µH. Use compression slugs (ferrite trimmer cores) for fine adjustment of small values without rewinding.

APPENDIX A — CALCULATIONS AND FORMULAS

Air-core solenoid inductance (Wheeler)L (µH) = (r2 × N2) / (9r + 10l) [r = radius cm, l = length cm, N = turns]
Toroidal inductanceL = μ0 × μr × N2 × A / l [A = cross-section area m², l = mean path length m]
Q factorQ = 2πfL / RDC
