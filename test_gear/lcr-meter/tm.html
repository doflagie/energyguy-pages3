CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers operation and calibration of a combined LCR meter measuring inductance (L), capacitance (C), and resistance (R) at test frequencies of 100 Hz, 1 kHz, and 10 kHz. The LCR meter is the primary instrument for verifying component values in RF circuits.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-003 (Capacitance Meter), TM-TGEAR-014 (Inductance Meter), TM-CAL-008 (Precision Resistors), TM-CAL-010 (Kelvin 4-Wire).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. AUTO-BALANCING BRIDGE

The auto-balancing bridge method applies a test voltage Vosc to the DUT and a current-sense resistor RS in series. The current I through the DUT is measured. Complex impedance Z = VDUT / I. The instrument decomposes Z into R + jX and computes L or C.

Series equivalent circuitZ = Rs + jωLs (inductor) or Z = Rs − j/(}ωCs) (capacitor)

2-2. Q AND D FACTORS

Q factor (inductor quality)Q = XL / Rs = ωL / Rs
D factor (dissipation, capacitor)D = Rs / XC = Rs × ωC

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Build test oscillator. Verify frequency with counter at each test frequency setting.
  2. Install precision current sense resistor RS. Measure actual value with 4-wire DMM and enter in firmware.
  3. Calibrate I/Q detector gain and phase balance per manufacturer data sheet.
  4. Perform open/short/load correction (OLOAD calibration) at each test frequency before use.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Open circuit calibration: measure Yopen at all test frequencies. Subtract from subsequent measurements.
  2. Short circuit calibration: measure Zshort at all test frequencies. Subtract from subsequent measurements.
  3. Load calibration (optional): measure precision 100 Ω ±0.01% resistor. Apply gain correction factor.
  4. Verify: 100 pF C0G reads 100 ±1 pF; 100 μH coil reads 100 ±2 μH; 100 Ω reads 100 ±0.2 Ω.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Phase balance of the I/Q detector affects accuracy for high-Q measurements. Adjust phase trim capacitor (2–10 pF) at the detector reference input for minimum Q error on a pure resistor (Q should read near zero for an ideal resistor).

APPENDIX A — CALCULATIONS AND FORMULAS

Inductance from Z at fL = Im(Z) / (2πf) [Im(Z) > 0]
Capacitance from Z at fC = −1 / (Im(Z) × 2πf) [Im(Z) < 0]
Dissipation factorD = 1/Q = Rs × 2πf × C
