CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of precision DC voltage references from 1.225 V to 10.000 V for calibrating DMMs, oscilloscopes, and ADCs. References are based on band-gap or buried-zener IC standards traceable to NIST via publicly available calibration data from the IC manufacturers.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-CAL-006 (Precision Voltage Reference), TM-TGEAR-017 (Fluke 15B+ DMM).

NOTEBand-gap references (LM4040, TL431, REF02) have initial tolerance of ±0.1% to ±2%. For calibration use, measure actual output voltage with a precision instrument and use measured value. Do NOT rely on nominal value as a calibration standard without individual measurement.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. BAND-GAP REFERENCE

The band-gap voltage of silicon (approximately 1.205 V at 0 K, extrapolated) is exploited by designing a circuit whose output voltage is proportional to VBE differences between two transistors operating at different current densities. The resulting band-gap voltage VBG ≈ 1.225 V has a temperature coefficient close to zero near room temperature.

Band-gap reference voltageVref ≈ VBG + (ΔVBE × scale factor) = 1.225 V (first-order)

2-2. BURIED ZENER REFERENCE

Buried-zener references (LM399, LTZ1000) use a subsurface zener diode structure with 1/f noise 100× lower than surface-breakdown zeners. They achieve stability of <1 ppm/month and TC <0.05 ppm/°C (LTZ1000 with oven). These are the highest-accuracy single-chip references available commercially.

Temperature coefficientTC = (dVref/dT) / Vref [ppm/°C]

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Solder LM4040 in SOT-23 on adapter PCB. Current-set resistor = (Vbat − Vref) / Iset. For 9 V bat and 1 mA: R = (9 − 4.1) / 0.001 = 4.9 kΩ. Use 4.7 kΩ + 470 Ω in series for easy adjustment.
  2. Allow 30-minute warmup with battery connected before measuring output. Thermal equilibrium reduces Vref variation.
  3. Measure output with precision DMM. Record: Vref_measured = _____. This is the working calibration value.
  4. Label reference module with measured Vref, date, and temperature during measurement.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

5-1. ABSOLUTE CALIBRATION

  1. Measure Vref with the most accurate available DMM (Fluke 15B+ or better).
  2. Record measured value, ambient temperature, battery voltage, and date.
  3. Traceability note: LM4040 manufacturer-characterized to NIST standards via wafer-level test. Initial tolerance ±0.1%; actual measured value is the calibration standard for this reference module.

5-2. TEMPERATURE COEFFICIENT MEASUREMENT

  1. Measure Vref at 25 °C (room temperature) and 45 °C (oven or heat gun from 30 cm).
  2. TC = (V45 − V25) / (V25 × 20 °C) × 106 ppm/°C. Accept <±200 ppm/°C for Class C.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

For shunt reference designs (LM4040), there is no adjustable output. Use an external amplifier (non-inverting op-amp) to scale Vref to other voltages. For instance, 4.096 V × (10 kΩ + 14.4 kΩ) / 10 kΩ = 10.000 V.

Scaling amplifier gainG = 1 + Rf/Rg; Vout = G × Vref
