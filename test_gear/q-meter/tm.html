CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers the design, construction, and calibration of a Q meter for measuring the quality factor Q of inductors and capacitors at HF frequencies (1–30 MHz). The Q meter enables selection of high-Q inductors for filters and matching networks, and verifies coil construction quality.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-014 (Inductance Meter), TM-TGEAR-015 (LCR Meter), TM-TGEAR-007 (GDO).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. SERIES RESONANCE METHOD

The DUT inductor L and a calibrated variable capacitor CV are connected in series with a low-impedance RF voltage source (Vinj). At resonance, the voltage across CV is Q times the source voltage:

Q from resonant voltageQ = VCV / Vinj

A high-impedance RF voltmeter reads VCV. Source Vinj is kept constant by the injector oscillator. Q is read directly from the ratio.

2-2. Q FACTOR DEFINITION

Q factorQ = 2πfrL / Rseries = 1 / (2πfrC × Rseries)

For RF coils wound on powdered-iron toroid: typical Q = 100–200 at 7 MHz. Air-core coils: Q = 200–500.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Wind injection coil: 2 turns 18 AWG silver-plated wire on 20 mm PTFE form. Measure RDC <0.05 Ω with 4-wire DMM.
  2. Calibrate variable capacitor CV against LCR meter. Record actual C per dial division.
  3. Align JFET voltmeter for linear response: apply known voltage from calibrated source, adjust meter calibration pot.
  4. Zero-set: short DUT terminals, tune CV to resonance with work coil, verify Q meter reads zero current (no circulating current).

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Connect known 100 μH inductor (low-loss, air core) at 7 MHz. Tune CV for maximum voltage reading. Record Q.
  2. Compare to LCR meter Q reading at 7 MHz. Values should agree within ±10%.
  3. Calibrate injected voltage Vinj: verify Vinj = 100 mV ±1% with calibrated RF voltmeter. Adjust injector attenuator.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Injection voltage stability is critical: Vinj drift of 1% causes 1% Q error. Use AGC on the injection oscillator (JFET gain control). Oscillator frequency is tuned by a calibrated dial referencing the variable capacitor.
