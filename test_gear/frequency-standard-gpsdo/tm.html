CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of a GPS-Disciplined Oscillator (GPSDO). The GPSDO produces a 10 MHz reference signal traceable to UTC/GPS time, enabling frequency measurement to <0.1 ppm. It serves as the master frequency standard for all other test equipment calibration in this library.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-CAL-002 (GPS 1PPS), TM-CAL-003 (GPSDO), TM-TGEAR-010 (Frequency Counter).

NOTEGPS accuracy depends on atmospheric conditions and satellite geometry. Under normal conditions, GPS 1PPS is accurate to <100 ns (0.1 µs). When GPS signal is lost, the OCXO holds frequency within ±0.5 ppm for up to 24 hours (holdover).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. PHASE-LOCKED LOOP DISCIPLINE

A voltage-controlled oscillator (VCXO or OCXO at 10 MHz) is phase-locked to the GPS 1 PPS signal. The DPLL (digital phase-locked loop) in firmware measures the phase offset between the oscillator's divided-down 1 Hz tick and the GPS 1 PPS. It applies a correction to the VCXO control voltage (EFC) to eliminate the error.

Phase errorφerr = (Tosc − TGPS) / TGPS
EFC voltage update (PI controller)VEFC = VEFC(prev) + Kp × φerr + Ki × Σφerr

2-2. LOOP FILTER DESIGN

The DPLL time constant τ is set to 100–300 seconds to attenuate GPS jitter (1–10 µs peak) while tracking low-frequency oscillator drift. Shorter τ tracks GPS noise; longer τ improves phase noise of output.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Install NEO-M8N on 3.3 V power rail. Verify 1 PPS output present (oscilloscope shows 1 Hz pulses, 100 ms high, 900 ms low).
  2. Install OCXO on separate 5 V regulated supply. Allow 30-minute warmup; output must be within ±5 ppm of 10 MHz.
  3. Connect EFC DAC output through 100 Ω series resistor to OCXO EFC pin. Verify DAC range 0–5 V tracks EFC input.
  4. Flash MCU firmware (open-source GPSDO firmware or custom DPLL). Connect GPS 1 PPS to interrupt pin.
  5. Power on. After first GPS lock, firmware begins accumulating phase measurements. Lock typically achieved in 5–15 minutes.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Allow 30-minute OCXO warmup before enabling DPLL.
  2. Enable GPS discipline. Monitor UART log output for phase error values; should converge to <100 ns within 10 minutes of lock.
  3. Connect 10 MHz output to second frequency counter with independent GPS reference. Verify displayed frequency agrees to <0.01 Hz difference over 60-second interval.
  4. Log EFC voltage every 10 minutes over 4 hours. EFC should be stable within ±0.1 V of lock-point, confirming thermal stability of OCXO.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Adjust PI loop constants Kp and Ki in firmware. Kp too large causes ringing; Ki too large causes slow drift. Start with τ = 200 s (Ki = 1/200). Optimal tuning minimizes ADEV (Allan deviation) at 1–100 second averaging time.
