CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of an RF power meter for measuring forward and reflected power at HF and VHF (1–150 MHz). A dual-directional coupler and a calibrated RF detector produce readings in watts and dBm.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-008 (Dummy Load), TM-TGEAR-027 (SWR Meter), TM-CAL-014 (Dummy Load Standard), TM-CAL-015 (Attenuator Pads).

CAUTIONThe directional coupler is rated for maximum 100 W continuous. Do not apply SSB peak power (typically 4× average) without accounting for PEP rating. Maximum safe PEP for 100 W average-rated couplers is 400 W.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. DIRECTIONAL COUPLER

A toroidal directional coupler (Sontheimer-Fredrick bridge or Bruene coupler) samples the forward and reflected voltage waves on the transmission line. Coupling factor Cf is typically −20 to −30 dB.

Coupled powerPcoupled = Pforward × 10Cf/10

2-2. RF DETECTOR

A Schottky diode (1N5711 or BAT85) rectifies the coupled RF signal. For small-signal operation (<200 mV input), the diode operates in the square-law region: output DC voltage is proportional to input RF power. For large signals, operation is in the linear region.

Detector output (square law)VDC = k × PRF [k = detector sensitivity constant, V/mW]

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Wind 10 turns of #28 enameled wire through FT-50-43 ferrite bead. This is the secondary; the through-line is the primary (1 turn).
  2. Install forward and reflected detector diodes per schematic. Verify diode orientation: forward detector anode to secondary, reflected detector anode to secondary with reversed polarity arm.
  3. Connect 4.7 kΩ loads and 100 pF bypass caps at each detector output.
  4. Connect meter movements or ADC to detector DC outputs.
  5. Apply 5 W at 14 MHz from calibrated source into dummy load. Measure forward detector voltage. Compute calibration constant K = P / VDC.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Connect power meter in line between known power source and precision 50 Ω dummy load (TM-CAL-014).
  2. Apply 1 W at 7 MHz. Adjust forward power calibration pot until display reads 1.00 W.
  3. Verify at 5 W and 10 W. Linearity error <±5%.
  4. Apply known signal generator output at −10 dBm (0.1 mW). For small-signal meter: adjust for 0.10 mW reading ±2%.
  5. Verify SWR accuracy: connect 2:1 SWR test load (100 Ω). SWR must read 2.0 ±0.1.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Frequency flatness of detector response is improved by using a low-capacitance Schottky diode. For HF-only use, response is flat within ±0.5 dB from 1 to 30 MHz. For VHF (50–150 MHz), add a compensation capacitor (5–10 pF) across the detector load resistor to pre-emphasize higher frequencies.
