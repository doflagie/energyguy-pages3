CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers the calibration and use of the NanoVNA two-port vector network analyzer for measuring S-parameters (S11, S21), antenna impedance, filter response, and transmission line characteristics from 50 kHz to 900 MHz. The NanoVNA uses SOLT calibration to remove systematic measurement errors.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-CAL-015 (Attenuator Pads), TM-CAL-014 (Dummy Load), TM-CAL-002 (GPS 1PPS), TM-TGEAR-022 (RF Attenuator Set).

NOTEThe NanoVNA internal oscillator is a Si5351A. Its frequency accuracy is ±30 ppm without correction. GPS frequency correction to <1 ppm is performed via the SOLT load calibration and a frequency counter cross-check.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. S-PARAMETER DEFINITIONS

S-parameters describe the linear behavior of a two-port network in terms of incident and reflected power waves:

S11 (input reflection)S11 = b1/a1 (with a2=0) — reflected wave at port 1
S21 (forward transmission)S21 = b2/a1 (with a2=0) — transmitted wave at port 2

2-2. 12-TERM ERROR MODEL

The NanoVNA implements a simplified 3-term (1-port) and 6-term (2-port) error model. SOLT calibration solves for error terms: directivity (ED), source match (ES), and reflection tracking (ER).

Corrected S11S11corrected = (S11measured − ED) / (ER + ES × (S11measured − ED))

2-3. SMITH CHART INTERPRETATION

The Smith chart maps complex reflection coefficient Γ onto a normalized impedance plane. Center = 50 Ω (Z0). Top arc = inductive reactance (+ jX). Bottom arc = capacitive reactance (− jX). Right = open (Z = ∞). Left = short (Z = 0).

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Construct SOLT kit (or use commercial NanoVNA cal kit). Mark each standard clearly: S/O/L/T.
  2. Power on NanoVNA. Allow 5-minute warmup. Verify firmware version (NanoVNA-D, NanoVNA-F, etc.).
  3. Connect to NanoVNA-QT or NanoVNA-App via USB for PC-based calibration management.
  4. Perform SOLT calibration at the measurement reference plane (cable end, not NanoVNA SMA ports).

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

5-1. SOLT ONE-PORT CALIBRATION (PORT 1)

  1. Set frequency range: 1 MHz to 300 MHz, 101 points.
  2. Connect SHORT to port 1. In NanoVNA-App: CAL → OPEN; wait for sweep to complete; press SHORT.
  3. Connect OPEN to port 1. Press OPEN.
  4. Connect LOAD (50 Ω) to port 1. Press LOAD.
  5. Press DONE / APPLY. Calibration is now active. Verify: short reads S11 = 1.0∠−180°; open reads S11 = 1.0∠0°; load reads S11 < −30 dB.

5-2. SOLT TWO-PORT CALIBRATION (PORTS 1 AND 2)

  1. After one-port calibration, connect THROUGH between port 1 and port 2. Press THRU.
  2. Press DONE. S21 through standard should read 0 dB ±0.2 dB from 1 to 300 MHz.

5-3. FREQUENCY AXIS CALIBRATION

  1. Tune NanoVNA to marker at 10.000 MHz. Connect frequency counter (TM-TGEAR-010) to port 1 via 20 dB attenuator.
  2. Record counter reading. Compute frequency error in ppm.
  3. Enter correction in NanoVNA firmware: CALIBRATE → FREQ CORRECTION (ppm).

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

The Si5351A reference crystal can be trimmed in firmware. Access via USB serial port or NanoVNA-App: "Calibrate → Crystal Correction." Typical trim range: ±200 ppm. After GPS-disciplined counter cross-check, enter the measured correction value.
