CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers calibration procedures for the Fluke 15B+ digital multimeter and equivalent instruments. The Fluke 15B+ provides DC voltage (0.1 mV – 1000 V), AC voltage (1 mV – 750 V), resistance (0.1 Ω – 40 MΩ), continuity, and frequency measurement. All calibration references are publicly traceable.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-CAL-006 (Precision Voltage Reference), TM-CAL-008 (Precision Resistors), TM-CAL-010 (Kelvin 4-Wire), TM-CAL-002 (GPS 1PPS).

WARNINGThe Fluke 15B+ is rated for CAT III 600 V and CAT II 1000 V. Observe probe ratings. Never measure AC mains circuits with probes rated below CAT III. The common (COM) lead is not isolated from the measurement circuit; do not float the instrument ground above chassis potential on mains-connected equipment.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. SIGMA-DELTA ADC

Modern DMMs use a sigma-delta (ΣΔ) ADC. An oversampled 1-bit stream is decimated to 4–5 significant digits. A precision band-gap voltage reference (e.g., LM4040, 1.225 V ±0.1%) establishes the measurement scale. All voltage ranges are scaled to this internal reference.

2-2. RESISTANCE MEASUREMENT

The DMM forces a known current through RX and measures the resulting voltage. On 2-wire measurement, lead resistance is included. On 4-wire (Kelvin) measurement, a separate pair of sense leads carries no current, eliminating lead resistance error.

4-wire resistanceRX = Vsense / Iforce

2-3. FREQUENCY MEASUREMENT

A comparator converts the input AC signal to a logic pulse train. A timer measures period T; frequency = 1/T. Accuracy depends on the oscillator reference, typically a 32.768 kHz crystal. Accuracy: ±0.5% for most DMMs; ±0.1% for Fluke 15B+.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Assemble 4.096 V reference on small PCB. Measure output with already-calibrated precision DMM if available.
  2. Allow 10-minute warmup for LM4040 before calibration measurement.
  3. Connect calibration reference to DMM DC V input. Record DMM reading vs. reference value.
  4. Note temperature during calibration; LM4040 TC is ±100 ppm/°C (class C) or ±50 ppm/°C (class A).

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

5-1. DC VOLTAGE CALIBRATION

  1. Connect 4.096 V reference to DMM DC V input (4 V range or 40 V range).
  2. Record DMM reading. Compute correction factor: K = Vref / VDMM.
  3. Apply K to all subsequent voltage measurements, or adjust DMM trim pot if user-accessible.
  4. Fluke 15B+ spec: ±0.5% + 2 digits on DC ranges. Verify DMM is within spec.

5-2. RESISTANCE CALIBRATION

  1. Connect precision 100 Ω ±0.01% resistor (TM-CAL-008). Use 4-wire (Kelvin) clips if available.
  2. Record reading. Error must be <±1 Ω on 400 Ω range.
  3. Connect 10 kΩ ±0.01% resistor. Record. Error must be <±100 Ω on 40 kΩ range.

5-3. FREQUENCY CALIBRATION

  1. Apply GPS-disciplined 1 kHz signal (TM-CAL-002). Record DMM Hz reading.
  2. Error must be <±0.5% (5 Hz at 1 kHz).
  3. Repeat at 10 kHz and 100 kHz.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

The Fluke 15B+ has user-inaccessible internal calibration adjustments. If the instrument is out of spec, it requires service by a qualified calibration lab. Log calibration correction factors in the instrument record for use in measurement uncertainty budgeting.
