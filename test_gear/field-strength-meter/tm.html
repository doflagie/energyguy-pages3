CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of an RF field strength meter for qualitative and semi-quantitative measurement of electromagnetic field intensity from 100 kHz to 300 MHz. Primary uses: antenna pattern assessment, transmitter output verification, and antenna proximity comparison.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-023 (RF Power Meter), TM-TGEAR-024 (RF Probe/Detector), TM-TGEAR-028 (TinySA Spectrum Analyzer).

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. SHORT ANTENNA AND DETECTOR

A short monopole or loop antenna intercepts a fraction of the incident electric or magnetic field. The induced voltage Vant across the antenna impedance is proportional to field strength E (V/m) for an electric-field antenna, or to flux density B for a loop.

Induced EMF in short dipoleVOC = E × heff [heff = effective height, meters]

2-2. DETECTOR CIRCUIT

An RF diode detector (1N60 germanium or BAT85 Schottky) rectifies the antenna signal. A 50–100 µA meter movement or op-amp with precision meter displays the rectified voltage. Response is proportional to E (linear detector) or E² (power detector).

2-3. LOOP VS. WHIP

A small loop antenna responds to the magnetic field H. At MF (AM broadcast), a ferrite loop is sensitive and directional. A quarter-wave whip is non-directional and responds to the electric field E. At VHF, a whip or 5-element Yagi provides directional capability for azimuth mapping.

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Solder detector diode with anode to antenna terminal. Keep lead length <10 mm.
  2. Connect 100 nF RF bypass capacitor from cathode to ground, followed by 100 kΩ shunt and meter in parallel.
  3. Install sensitivity pot in series with meter: allows full-scale deflection at different field levels.
  4. House in metal enclosure (Altoids tin or aluminum box). Connect enclosure to detector circuit ground.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Set up known transmitter (TM-TGEAR-026 signal generator) at fixed distance d from antenna.
  2. Calculate approximate field at distance d: E = √(30 × PERP) / d (V/m).
  3. Adjust sensitivity pot until meter reads FSD at this computed field.
  4. Mark scale: FSD = computed E value. Scale is linear for small-signal detector operation.
  5. Cross-check with TinySA: point probe at same distance as field strength meter and compare relative readings.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

For frequency-selective operation, add a tuned circuit (L-C bandpass filter) between antenna and detector. This suppresses off-frequency signals and increases sensitivity at the tuned frequency by approximately 10 dB (Q factor gain).
