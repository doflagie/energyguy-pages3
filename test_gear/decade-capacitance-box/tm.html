CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers construction and calibration of a decade capacitance box providing switchable capacitance in 1 pF steps from 1 pF to 1111.1 nF. Used to set known capacitance values for oscillator design, filter alignment, and calibrating capacitance meters.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-003 (Capacitance Meter), TM-TGEAR-015 (LCR Meter), TM-CAL-011 (RC Time Constant).

NOTEDecade capacitance boxes have limited high-frequency performance due to lead inductance. Self-resonance of the switches and wiring limits usable range to <1 MHz for most designs.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. SWITCHED PARALLEL CAPACITORS

Each decade consists of nine capacitors: one at value C (1×), one at 2C, one at 3C (or series/parallel combination), etc. Decade switches connect capacitors in parallel. Total capacitance is the sum of all switched-in capacitors.

Parallel capacitanceCtotal = C1 + C2 + C3 + ... + Cn

2-2. SELF-RESONANCE

Lead inductance Llead (typically 10–20 nH per switch contact) creates a self-resonant frequency with each capacitor. Above fSRF, the capacitor appears inductive.

Self-resonant frequencyfSRF = 1 / (2π × √(Llead × C))

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Measure each capacitor individually with LCR meter. Sort by value; use closest pairs for critical decades.
  2. Mount capacitors on rotary switch decks. Use phenolic or PTFE switch for high-Q performance.
  3. Keep all wiring under 20 mm. Use solid copper bus bars for the common return where possible.
  4. House in metal enclosure. Connect enclosure to circuit ground to screen external fields from small capacitances.
  5. Mark enclosure with calibration date and traceable standard used.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Zero the LCR meter with leads and test clips connected but box terminals open.
  2. Set decade box to minimum (all switches to zero).
  3. Zero-correct LCR meter for stray capacitance of box wiring (typically 2–10 pF).
  4. Set each switch position 1–9 of each decade. Record measured vs. expected value. Accept ±2% or ±1 digit.
  5. Record cumulative value (e.g., all switches at 9) and compare to sum of individual values.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

Individual capacitor values cannot be adjusted after construction. Replace any capacitor measuring outside tolerance. For critical applications, measure total at each setting and record a correction table.
