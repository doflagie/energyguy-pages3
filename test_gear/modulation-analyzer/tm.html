CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL INFORMATION

1-1. SCOPE

This manual covers design, construction, and calibration of a modulation analyzer for measuring amplitude modulation (AM) depth and frequency deviation (FM deviation) in RF transmitters. Covering 500 kHz to 170 MHz, it verifies transmitter compliance and modulation quality.

1-2. APPLICABLE DOCUMENTS

TM-TGEAR-028 (TinySA Spectrum Analyzer), TM-TGEAR-026 (Signal Generator), TM-CAL-004 (FM Broadcast Reference).

CAUTIONMaximum RF input at the modulation analyzer is typically +20 dBm (100 mW). Use a 20 dB attenuator pad when measuring transmitter outputs above 100 mW.

CHAPTER 2 — THEORY OF OPERATION

2-1. AM MODULATION DEPTH

AM depth m (modulation index) is defined as:

AM modulation depthm = (Vmax − Vmin) / (Vmax + Vmin) × 100%

An envelope detector (diode + RC filter) recovers the modulating signal. Peak-to-peak measurement of the envelope gives Vmax and Vmin.

2-2. FM DEVIATION

FM deviation is measured via a discriminator (Foster-Seeley, ratio detector, or PLL). The discriminator output voltage is proportional to instantaneous frequency deviation Δf.

FM modulation indexβ = Δf / fm [Δf = peak deviation, fm = modulating freq]

CHAPTER 4 — ASSEMBLY PROCEDURES

  1. Align IF ceramic filter at 10.7 MHz with signal generator and spectrum analyzer.
  2. Calibrate AM envelope detector: apply 100% AM modulated carrier. Adjust RC time constant for flat audio response 300 Hz – 3 kHz.
  3. Calibrate FM discriminator: set PLL free-running frequency to IF center (10.7 MHz). Trim VCO to center.
  4. Verify FM deviation sensitivity: 75 kHz deviation (broadcast standard) should produce 1 kHz audio at calibration level.

CHAPTER 5 — CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

5-1. AM CALIBRATION

  1. Apply signal from calibrated AM generator: 1 MHz carrier, 1 kHz tone, 30% modulation (m = 0.30).
  2. Adjust AM meter scale to read 30% at this modulation depth.
  3. Verify at 100% modulation (m = 1.00): meter should read 100% ±2%.

5-2. FM DEVIATION CALIBRATION

  1. Tune to an FM broadcast station (e.g., 98.3 MHz). Broadcast stereo pilot = 19 kHz, max deviation = ±75 kHz.
  2. Adjust FM meter scale using broadcast standard as reference per TM-CAL-004.
  3. Verify with 2-meter CTCSS tone test: 67.0 Hz CTCSS at ±5 kHz deviation should read 5 kHz.

CHAPTER 6 — TUNING AND ADJUSTMENT

PLL capture range must exceed maximum FM deviation. Set loop filter bandwidth 3× wider than maximum modulating frequency. For VHF broadcast, loop BW > 100 kHz. For narrow-band 2m (5 kHz deviation), loop BW > 10 kHz.
